## D'Andre Swift — RB, CHI, bye 10  (player_id: 6790)

- **Role:** starter (lead back in a committee with Kyle Monangai)
- **Situation:** Coming off arguably his best season — RB16 in fantasy points per game, 257 touches, 1,386 total yards, career highs in rushing yards and rushing TDs, and a 61.3% opportunity share. Chicago made no significant RB additions, so the same split returns. From Weeks 10-18, after Monangai's emergence, Swift held the passing-down role with a 43% route share to Monangai's 28.8% and was still the RB20 in PPG, but red zone rushes were split nearly evenly (18 to 19). Ben Johnson's offense returns Caleb Williams and six of last year's top eight target-getters; D.J. Moore (85 targets) was traded to Buffalo and Olamide Zaccheaus left for Atlanta, which could funnel a few extra targets to the backfield. Offensive line is the question — LT Ozzy Trapilo ruptured a patellar tendon in the Wild Card win.
- **Health:** No current injury reported. He played all but one game in 2025 and dealt with a groin issue late in that season that did not cost him Week 16. Monangai was reported by Ben Johnson to be dealing with a soft-tissue injury expected to be "on the shorter side."
- **Risk:** The committee tips further toward Monangai in his second year, particularly at the goal line, where they already split evenly. That's the specific failure mode: Swift keeps the passing-down role but loses the touchdowns, and 14-15 PPG becomes 10-11. There's also a genuine ceiling problem — even the bull case doesn't get him much past his RB16 finish.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR his receiving role is worth real but not enormous points, so he profiles as a steady RB2/flex with a high floor and modest ceiling. His RB23-ish ADP is below last year's finish; that's the value. Take him as your second or third back, not as an RB1.
- **Confidence:** high
