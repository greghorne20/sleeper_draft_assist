## Jayden Daniels — QB, WAS, bye 7  (player_id: 11566)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Daniels returns healthy after a 2025 that fell apart — seven games played (two unfinished), 1,262 pass yards, 8 TD, 3 INT, 278 rush yards. Washington went 5-12 and fired the Kliff Kingsbury offense. The supporting cast was upgraded: Stefon Diggs joins Terry McLaurin, and Rachaad White (40-of-45 on targets in 2025) arrives as a genuine checkdown outlet. His 2024 rookie ceiling — a top-5 fantasy QB finish — is the reason the price is what it is.
- **Health:** Reported healthy and running camp; the Commanders described his strongest practice of camp on Aug. 4. In 2025 he dealt with a sprained knee (Week 2), a strained hamstring (Week 7) and a dislocated elbow (Week 9) that re-aggravated in Week 14.
- **Risk:** The offensive line in front of him. As of late August, LT Laremy Tunsil was out after triceps surgery, C Nick Allegretti had a calf issue and RG Sam Cosmi missed practice; a Commanders beat writer called the line "inoperable" and the offense the worst she'd seen in years. Washington signed D.J. Humphries as a stopgap. A running QB behind a broken line is exactly how last season happened.
- **Draft note:** His rushing is what makes him worth anything in a 4-point-passing-TD league — that's where his edge over pocket passers actually shows up. Round 5–7 is the range; I'd take him over Burrow in this scoring.
- **Confidence:** high
