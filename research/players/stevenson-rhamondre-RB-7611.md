## Rhamondre Stevenson — RB, NE, bye 11  (player_id: 7611)

- **Role:** starter — listed first on New England's initial unofficial depth chart and the current front-runner for lead-back duties
- **Situation:** He out-snapped TreVeyon Henderson through the first half of 2025 before a midseason turf toe injury opened the door for the rookie; they split closer to 50/50 down the stretch, and then Stevenson took over in the playoffs, out-carrying Henderson 58-30 and out-rushing him 217-76 across four games at a 70.1% snap share. Regular-season line: 130 carries for 603 yards and 7 TDs in 14 games, plus 32 catches for 345 yards and 2 TDs — RB21 in PPG. He beat Henderson in essentially every efficiency metric: fifth in explosive run rate, missed tackle rate and yards after contact per attempt, and 1.41 yards per route run to Henderson's 0.98, plus better pass protection. Henderson's pass protection is still described as a work in progress. Drake Maye is the quarterback.
- **Health:** Practicing and healthy, drawing praise all camp. The relevant injury is Henderson's: he hurt his right ankle slipping in practice Aug. 24, underwent precautionary tests, had not been cleared for full participation as of Aug. 31 and did not practice Sept. 1. One report says Henderson should be "good to go" for Week 1 vs. Seattle; either way Stevenson has taken the key backfield reps since.
- **Risk:** Regression plus a healthy Henderson. Stevenson's 2025 was a career year in efficiency — 4.6 YPC up from 3.9, a career-high nine total scores — and his late-season fantasy numbers (26.8 PPR PPG over the final three regular-season games) are flatly unrepeatable. If Henderson takes the year-two leap the market expects and fixes the pass protection, Stevenson's role compresses fast. He's also had fumbling issues.
- **Draft note:** RB28 ADP (around pick 78, Round 6-7) for the likely Week 1 starter in a good offense is the value here. In 0.5 PPR his 30+ catch role and goal-line work give him a usable floor. Solid RB3/flex with standalone value and immediate upside if Henderson's ankle lingers.
- **Confidence:** high
