## Kaleb Johnson — RB, GB, bye 11  (player_id: 12504)
- **Role:** backup, but in a backfield with a wide-open lead job
- **Situation:** Traded from Pittsburgh to Green Bay on Aug 30, 2026 for a 2028 sixth-rounder. He was buried in Pittsburgh behind Jaylen Warren and new signing Rico Dowdle under Mike McCarthy and looked like a cut candidate (3.1 YPC on 17 preseason carries). He lands in a Packers room in chaos: Josh Jacobs was placed on the Commissioner's Exempt List the same day over misdemeanor battery and criminal damage charges, with no timetable for return. MarShawn Lloyd — healthy through camp for the first time — sits atop the depth chart, with special-teamer Chris Brooks behind him. Johnson arrived days before Week 1 and is learning the offense cold.
- **Health:** No current injury reported. He missed three games at Iowa in 2023 with injuries; nothing recent found. UNKNOWN beyond "active."
- **Risk:** He's the fourth guy in a room where the front-runner (Lloyd) has two injury-shortened seasons behind him — but Johnson was traded for a sixth because he was bad, not because he was blocked. A 2.5 YPC rookie year and a scheme he learned in a week is a thin base. And Jacobs could be reinstated at any point and erase the whole thing.
- **Draft note:** Last-round lottery ticket in half-PPR — the Jacobs situation makes the Green Bay backfield the single most volatile in the league. In a 13-round league I'd let him go and be first in on waivers if Lloyd goes down.
- **Confidence:** medium
