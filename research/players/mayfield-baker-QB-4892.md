## Baker Mayfield — QB, TB, bye 10  (player_id: 4892)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Unquestioned Week 1 starter, playing out the final year of his three-year, $100M deal after extension talks collapsed at the start of camp (GM Jason Licht says there are no immediate plans to restart them). He started all 17 games in 2025 for 3,693 yards, 26 TD, 11 INT plus 382 rushing yards and a score, finishing QB17 in points per game after being QB4 in 2024. Big changes: Mike Evans left for San Francisco, Josh Grizzard was fired, and Zac Robinson was hired as OC reportedly with a push from Mayfield himself (they overlapped with the Rams in 2022). Robinson's two Atlanta offenses both ran more than they passed, so expect balance rather than volume. The receiving corps is now Emeka Egbuka, Chris Godwin, Jalen McMillan and Cade Otton, with Bucky Irving and offseason addition Kenny Gainwell in the backfield.
- **Health:** No current designation. He sat out the preseason opener as a precaution and was expected to play the following week. Last season he played through a sprained MCL/PCL after Week 2, plus a bicep tendon strain and a left shoulder issue — worth noting for a QB whose fantasy value leans on rushing.
- **Risk:** The 2025 collapse repeating. He was QB4 through six weeks and QB22 the rest of the way, and the Evans departure removes his highest-value red-zone target. His two best rushing seasons came at 30 and 31, which is not how QB rushing usually ages. If Egbuka's toe lingers and Godwin isn't 100%, this becomes a low-volume, run-first offense with a QB2 attached.
- **Draft note:** In a 1-QB, 4-point-passing-TD league he's a late-round QB2 you pair with someone else, not a set-and-forget starter. Round 11+ or stream him — Week 1 vs. Cincinnati is a legitimately good spot.
- **Confidence:** high
