## Caleb Williams — QB, CHI, bye 10  (player_id: 11560)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Year 3, and Year 2 in Ben Johnson's offense. Williams finished inside the top 12 fantasy QBs in 2025, nearly became the first Bear to throw for 4,000 yards, led Chicago to 12 wins and a playoff victory, and closed the year (including the postseason) with seven straight multi-passing-TD games. His weapons are legitimately good: Rome Odunze, Colston Loveland, Luther Burden III, D'Andre Swift. He also ran for nearly 500 yards as a rookie and posted nine games last season with a 10+ yard rush.
- **Health:** No injury reported. He's been practicing normally through camp and joint practices with Tennessee.
- **Risk:** The accuracy hasn't arrived. He completed 58.1% of his passes last season — that's the whole bear case. His fantasy line was carried by a 4.8% TD rate that looks league-normal rather than repeatable-upward, and if that regresses without a completion-rate jump, he's a low-end QB1 who needs rushing weeks to matter.
- **Draft note:** ADP around Round 8 (76–88 overall). In 4-point-passing-TD scoring his legs are what separate him, and at that price he's a fine one-and-done QB1 — take him a round after the tier above him rather than reaching.
- **Confidence:** high
