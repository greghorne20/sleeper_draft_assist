## Jeremiyah Love — RB, ARI, bye 14  (player_id: 13287)

- **Role:** starter — but Arizona's published depth chart listed Tyler Allgeier first, and beat coverage describes a 1A/1B
- **Situation:** Love went No. 3 overall in the 2026 draft, the highest-drafted back since Saquon Barkley in 2018, after winning the Doak Walker Award at Notre Dame (1,372 rush yards, 18 TD, 27 catches as a junior). Arizona's first depth chart of the summer had Allgeier as RB1 and Love as RB2 — normal for an unproven rookie, but worth noting. James Conner opened on injured reserve with a return designation and will miss at least four games; Trey Benson has been a non-factor. The environment is the problem: Kyler Murray was released in March, Jacoby Brissett is the Week 1 starter, the Cardinals had the second-worst rushing offense in the league last year (93.1 yards/game), and their rookie guard is out for the season.
- **Health:** High ankle sprain suffered in the preseason opener on Aug. 18 (he got bent awkwardly on his last of 14 touches). No surgery required. He missed the final two preseason games. Mike Garafolo reported he's "about 50/50" for the Week 1 opener vs. the Chargers; HC Mike LaFleur has stayed noncommittal while saying Love is progressing. Unresolved as of today.
- **Risk:** The offense, more than the injury. Even fully healthy, a rookie back on a projected bottom-tier team behind a shaky line and a caretaker QB has a low touchdown ceiling — and Allgeier will take real carries. If the ankle costs him September, the redraft season gets short.
- **Draft note:** Talent is worth a Round 2–3 swing, but the ecosystem plus the ankle argue for letting him fall to Round 3 in this format. He's the kind of pick that needs a bench spot spent on Allgeier alongside him.
- **Confidence:** medium — role and injury both unsettled going into the opener.
