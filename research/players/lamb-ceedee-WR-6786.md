## CeeDee Lamb — WR, DAL, bye 14  (player_id: 6786)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Lamb remains Dallas' primary outside/slot alpha and has held the No. 1 WR job since 2021, but George Pickens' 2025 breakout (93-1,429-9) genuinely muddies the pecking order — Lamb went for 1,077 yards in 14 games and the two combined for 2,506 yards. Pickens signed the $27.3M franchise tag and returns for 2026, and Jake Ferguson led the team with 24 red-zone targets last year, so Lamb is sharing high-value looks in a passing offense that finished second in the NFL in yards (4,527). Brian Schottenheimer's staff describes a "dramatically revamped roster" but the core skill group is intact.
- **Health:** No current injury. He missed three games in 2025 with injury and says he hasn't felt this good in two years; he's been practicing fully and playing in camp/joint practices through late August.
- **Risk:** Touchdown famine repeating. He scored three TDs on 11 end-zone targets (6.6 expected) in 2025 and led all receivers with eight drops. If Ferguson keeps the red-zone role and Pickens keeps drawing the same volume, Lamb is a high-floor yardage receiver without the scoring spike his draft cost implies.
- **Draft note:** In half-PPR with a 1-WR-heavy roster (2 WR + FLEX), he's a Round 1–2 target; the 0.5 PPR setting slightly dulls his reception-volume edge relative to full PPR, so I'd want him at the back of Round 1 rather than the top.
- **Confidence:** high
