## Jahmyr Gibbs — RB, DET, bye 6  (player_id: 9221)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Signed a three-year, $67.5M extension ($51.5M guaranteed) on Aug. 6, 2026 after a brief hold-in that cost him the first week of camp; it became official Aug. 7. - **Confidence:** high
