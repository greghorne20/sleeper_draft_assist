## Brashard Smith — RB, KC, bye 5  (player_id: 12455)

- **Role:** backup — RB3 on the initial 53, primarily a kick returner
- **Situation:** Kansas City remade the backfield around Kenneth Walker III as the new lead back. Smith made the initial 53 with Walker and rookie Emmett Johnson, but he was listed fourth on the unofficial preseason depth chart behind Walker, Emari Demercado and Johnson, and the reporting was that the order behind Walker was unsettled. His clearest current value to the team is on special teams — he had a 57-yard kick return this summer and spoke to the media in September specifically about his special-teams role. As a rookie he played 17% of offensive snaps with 44 carries and 35 targets, so the receiving usage is real but small; he finished the year with 95 rushing yards on the season at 3.0 per carry and one receiving TD. He did get one look as the lead back in Week 18 with Pacheco resting (12 carries, 56 yards) and produced chunk gains of 14, 10 and 9.
- **Health:** No injury designation found. He made the 53 and was practicing and speaking to media in early September.
- **Risk:** He's the third back on a team that just imported a starter, and he wasn't efficient as a rookie (3.43 YPC, 4.9 yards per target). Even the injury path doesn't clear for him — the reporting is that he'd likely share a backfield with Johnson if Walker missed time rather than inherit it. At 5'10", 194 he's not the between-the-tackles answer, so the realistic ceiling is a change-of-pace passing-down role that Kansas City hasn't committed to him.
- **Draft note:** Not draftable in a 13-round, 12-team league with six bench spots. This is a waiver-wire name to file away in case Walker gets hurt, not a pick.
- **Confidence:** medium — his roster spot and depth-chart position are well confirmed; what he'd actually do with opportunity is unproven.
