## Jonathan Taylor — RB, IND, bye 13  (player_id: 6813)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Signed a two-year, $44M extension on Aug. 6, 2026 and was named a team captain. He led the NFL in 2025 with 323 carries, 1,585 rush yards and 18 rushing TDs while setting career receiving highs (46 rec, 378 yds, ~10.5% target share). Daniel Jones returns from a torn Achilles; the RB2 job is an unsettled Giddens/McGowan battle, though the staff is publicly split on trimming Taylor's workload.
- **Health:** Healthy. Rested through the entire 2026 preseason as precaution, not injury; played all 17 games in 2025. Chronic ankle issues are the historical, not current, concern.
- **Risk:** Injury history (missed games 2022–2024) plus the efficiency dip to 3.3 YPC over his final seven 2025 games once Jones went down — a stacked-box season is the failure mode.
- **Draft note:** Early Round 1; the growing receiving role adds half-PPR value — worth 1.04–1.08.
- **Confidence:** high
