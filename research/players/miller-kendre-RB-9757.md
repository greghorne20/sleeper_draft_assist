## Kendre Miller — RB, NO, bye 8  (player_id: 9757)
- **Role:** committee — nominally RB3, but effectively the No. 2 back to open the season
- **Situation:** Made New Orleans' initial 53 as one of four backs (Travis Etienne Jr., Alvin Kamara, Miller, Audric Estimé), after being an unlikely roster lock — he missed camp days with a back injury but performed once healthy. The room is beaten up: Kamara is out at least a month with a sprained MCL, Devin Neal is on IR, and Estimé has also been dinged. Etienne, signed this offseason, is the lead back with Kamara nominally splitting when healthy. Miller also handles kickoff returns. Career line is modest: 497 rushing yards at 3.9 across three injury-shortened seasons. Tyler Shough at QB.
- **Health:** Missed several practices in August with a back injury and was described as healthy again by late preseason. He has an extensive injury history — his 2025 season ended early. No current designation found beyond a "questionable" tag on one ESPN page; current status: probably healthy, but not confirmed clean.
- **Risk:** Durability is the whole story — he has never stayed on the field for a full season, and the moment Kamara is back from the MCL he's the third option again in a weak offense. Etienne is the clear No. 1.
- **Draft note:** The most interesting immediate opportunity of the running backs in this batch because of Kamara's absence. Still only a last-round or waiver-priority play in a 13-round half-PPR league — he's not a receiving back, so the 0.5 PPR doesn't help him much. Take him ahead of Claiborne and Kaleb Johnson if you want one Week 1-relevant backup.
- **Confidence:** medium
