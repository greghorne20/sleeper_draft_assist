## Jaxon Smith-Njigba — WR, SEA, bye 11  (player_id: 9488)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Reigning AP Offensive Player of the Year and unanimous first-team All-Pro, leading the NFL with 1,793 receiving yards on a franchise-record 119 catches and 163 targets in Seattle's Super Bowl LX season. New OC Brian Fleury is a first-time play-caller; Sam Darnold returns at QB, with Cooper Kupp and Rashid Shaheed also in the room.

- **Risk:** Seattle threw the fourth-fewest passes in the league in 2025 — a run-leaning first-time play-caller plus Kupp and Shaheed could cap raw targets, and the 10-TD total is regression-prone.
- **Draft note:** Top-5 WR with an elite half-PPR volume floor — worth a mid-to-late Round 1 pick.
- **Confidence:** high
