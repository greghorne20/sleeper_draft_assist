## Brock Bowers — TE, LV, bye 13  (player_id: 11604)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Bowers is the engine of the Las Vegas passing game and has finished as a top-three fantasy TE in both of his NFL seasons (15.5 and 14.7 PPG). He set the rookie reception record with 112 catches for 1,194 yards in 2024, then went 64-680-7 in 12 games in 2025 while hurt, still posting a 21.7% target share and 6.9 targets per game — second among tight ends. The receiver room around him is thin: Jalen Nailor (career highs of 29 catches, 444 yards), Tre Tucker and Jack Bech. New HC/play-caller Klint Kubiak funneled volume to Jaxon Smith-Njigba in Seattle last season. Kirk Cousins opens at QB, with No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza behind him; camp reports highlight the Cousins–Bowers connection.
- **Health:** He hurt his knee (sprained PCL) in Week 1 of 2025, played through it, and went on IR with two games left for a season-ending procedure — Pelissero reported the move was to get him "100% in 2026." He entered the offseason saying the knee was near 100% and has been called the star of camp, making a big play in seemingly every practice. Preseason snaps were limited by choice, not by the knee.
- **Risk:** The knee not holding up over 17 games, and the quarterback. He was a boom-bust asset even at elite usage last year — 24.8% of his season PPR points came in one game against Jacksonville, and he ranked TE14 or worse in five weeks. A Cousins-to-Mendoza midseason switch would add variance.
- **Draft note:** Late Round 2 / early Round 3. Your league has a required TE slot with no TE premium, so you're paying for scarcity rather than scoring bonuses — that argues for taking him at the back of that range rather than the front.
- **Confidence:** high
