## Lamar Jackson — QB, BAL, bye 13  (player_id: 4881)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Fully healthy entering 2026 after hamstring and back injuries cost him four games and dropped him to career lows in rushing (67 attempts, 349 yards) in 2025. New HC Jesse Minter and OC Declan Doyle are installing a more explosive-play offense; he was sharp in camp, with Zay Flowers, Rashod Bateman and Derrick Henry around him. Baltimore opens at Indianapolis on Sept. 13.
- **Health:** Reported fully healthy. A minor camp foot/step-on scare resolved with no lingering issue reported entering Week 1.
- **Risk:** With 4-point passing TDs his edge is almost entirely rushing production — if 2025's suppressed rushing volume was a coaching choice rather than an injury artifact, he's merely a good QB1 rather than a positional advantage.
- **Draft note:** Top-2 QB with the highest rushing ceiling, but the format compresses the position — rounds 3–5 if you want an elite QB.
- **Confidence:** high
