## Malik Washington — WR, MIA, bye 6  (player_id: 11610)
- **Role:** starter (currently Miami's WR1 by default) plus primary punt and kick returner
- **Situation:** The single biggest role change in this batch. Miami released Tyreek Hill and traded Jaylen Waddle to Denver, gutting the room. Washington is listed as a first-team receiver on Miami's depth chart alongside rookie Caleb Douglas and veteran Jalen Tolbert, and he opened the preseason with the starters, catching Miami's first two offensive plays. New staff (Jeff Hafley HC, Bobby Slowik OC) with Malik Willis at QB — Willis averaged a league-best 12.1 Y/A in four 2025 appearances, a big jump from Tua's 6.9. 2025 line: 46-317-3 on 65 targets, plus 17 carries for 110 and a TD.
- **Health:** No injury reported; he played all 17 games in 2025 and worked through camp and preseason with the first team.
- **Risk:** He's the WR1 because there's nobody else, not because he earned it — 317 receiving yards on 62-65 targets is genuinely bad efficiency, and he's a 5-foot-8 slot in an offense that will run through De'Von Achane. Rookie Caleb Douglas had a strong summer and Chris Bell is coming back from an ACL; either could take the job by midseason.
- **Draft note:** Late-round flier with real half-PPR appeal because his value is reception-driven and he's the returner (your league pays 6 for `st_td` but nothing for return yards). Around the ADP-148 range he's a bench pick, not a starter — in 13 rounds that's your last pick or a waiver add.
- **Confidence:** medium
