## Justin Herbert — QB, LAC, bye 7  (player_id: 6797)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Entrenched starter in a materially improved situation. Greg Roman was fired and replaced as offensive coordinator by former Dolphins head coach Mike McDaniel, a change every outlet frames as a significant upgrade in explosiveness and pass-game design. The offensive line, which was bottom-two in PFF's blocking grades and allowed a league-high 43.6% pressure rate on Herbert, gets Rashawn Slater and Joe Alt back — the pair combined for six games in 2025 — plus additions Cole Strange and Tyler Biadasz. Weapons: Ladd McConkey, Quentin Johnston, Tre Harris, TE Oronde Gadsden and RB Omarion Hampton; Keenan Allen is gone. He finished QB10 in 2025 (3,727 yards, 26 TD, 13 INT) on a career-high 498 rushing yards. The Alt split is striking: 23.9 PPG in five games with him, 16.2 without.
- **Health:** No reported injury. He played a series in the preseason finale and has been doing normal camp work; the one flagged note was a planned rest day from throwing.
- **Risk:** Two things. He averaged only about 32 pass attempts per game under Jim Harbaugh, who is still the head coach — a new coordinator does not automatically change the team's run-first identity. And the offensive line health that drove his 2025 collapse is the same variable again; the Chargers have had recurring line injuries every preseason, including a Slater scare in August.
- **Draft note:** In a 1QB league with 4-pt passing TDs, he's a QB6–10 you take late rather than a target; the rushing floor (31.1 yards/game) is what makes him more than a streamer here.
- **Confidence:** high
