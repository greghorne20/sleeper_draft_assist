## Malik Nabers — WR, NYG, bye 8  (player_id: 11632)

- **Role:** starter (Week 1 availability unresolved)
- **Situation:** New York's clear alpha when healthy, and the Giants did not place him on IR or PUP before roster cuts, which preserves Week 1 eligibility. He tore his ACL and meniscus on September 28, 2025 and needed a second procedure this offseason to remove scar tissue. He practiced throughout camp, shed the red no-contact jersey, and has taken team-period reps, but did not play in preseason. Around him: Jaxson Dart in year two as the undisputed starter under new HC John Harbaugh and play-caller Matt Nagy, with Darius Slayton, Darnell Mooney, Odell Beckham Jr., Isaiah Likely and rookie Malachi Fields as the supporting cast. As a rookie in 2024 he went 109-1,204-7 in 15 games.
- **Health:** Recovering from ACL/meniscus, ~11 months post-injury. Harbaugh said it is "reasonable to assume" he plays Week 1 against Dallas on September 13, but as of this week Nabers himself would not commit, and Harbaugh has called his practice participation day-to-day. One analysis frames the worst case as missing two or three games.
- **Risk:** Two distinct failure modes. He could miss the opening weeks outright, or — more insidious for a redraft roster — he could play on a snap count and route restriction for a month and deliver WR3 production at a WR1 price. This is a run-leaning Harbaugh offense with Cam Skattebo and Tyrone Tracy, so the passing volume ceiling isn't guaranteed either.
- **Draft note:** A discount asset whose price should already reflect the risk; worth taking as a WR2 with upside if he slides, but he isn't a player to build the roster around given 6 bench spots and no IR relief mentioned in the league settings.
- **Confidence:** medium — the reporting is thorough and current, but his actual Week 1 status is genuinely unresolved as of today
