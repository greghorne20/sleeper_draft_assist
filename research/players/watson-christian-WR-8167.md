## Christian Watson — WR, GB, bye 11  (player_id: 8167)

- **Role:** starter — listed atop Green Bay's WR depth chart
- **Situation:** Watson came back from a January 2025 ACL tear to post 35 catches on 55 targets for 611 yards and 6 TDs in 10 games, averaging 17.5 yards per catch and topping a 70% snap share in five straight games at one point. The Packers then paid him — reporting varies between a four-year $92M and a four-year $110.5M extension signed in June — and cleared the room in front of him: Romeo Doubs left in free agency and Dontayvion Wicks was traded, vacating roughly 127–131 combined targets and a 31% target share. He sits ahead of Jayden Reed, Matthew Golden, Bo Melton, Skyy Moore and J. Michael Sturdivant; Savion Williams is on IR-return (ankle) and eligible around Week 5. Josh Jacobs opens the season on the commissioner's exempt list, which should push this offense to throw more than it wants to.
- **Health:** Healthy entering Week 1 and roughly 20 months removed from the ACL tear. He has never played a full 17-game season — game totals of 14, 9, 15 and 10 across four years — and a concussion interrupted his December last season.
- **Risk:** Availability, plainly. He has missed time in all four NFL seasons and has never topped 41 catches or 620 receiving yards. Beyond that, Matt LaFleur's offense historically rotates who the top option is week to week, and a healthy Tucker Kraft plus Reed and Golden means the target concentration Watson needs may not materialize. His deep-heavy role also makes him boom-or-bust: he scored 18+ four times in 2025 and averaged 7.7 in his other six outings.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR the low catch volume hurts less than in full PPR but more than in standard — he's a volatile WR3/flex with WR2 upside, roughly a fifth-to-sixth-round pick. Best used as a swing piece with a stable WR2 behind him, not as a weekly must-start.
- **Confidence:** high
