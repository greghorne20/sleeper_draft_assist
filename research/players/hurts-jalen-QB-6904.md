## Jalen Hurts — QB, PHI, bye 10  (player_id: 6904)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Entrenched starter, but in a substantially reworked offense. Kevin Patullo was fired after one season and Sean Mannion — a first-time play-caller bringing a Shanahan/McVay-style system — takes over, which multiple Philadelphia writers describe as the most significant scheme change of the Sirianni era. A.J. Brown was traded to New England, leaving DeVonta Smith as WR1 alongside Makai Lemon, Dontayvion Wicks, Elijah Moore and Marquise Brown, with Dallas Goedert re-signed. Hurts finished QB8 in 2025 for the second straight year despite a career-high 25 passing TDs, because his rushing collapsed: 105 carries, 421 yards, eight rushing TDs, against a four-year average of about 13 rushing scores. Mannion said in August the offense will keep leaning on the QB sneak package, and camp reports have described Hurts looking quicker.
- **Health:** No reported injury entering the season.
- **Risk:** The floor and ceiling both live in his legs. If designed-run and goal-line volume stay at 2025 levels — Philadelphia was 27th in team pass attempts — he's a low-end QB1 in a 4-pt-passing-TD league. There's also real friction risk: ESPN reporting this spring described Hurts as resistant to coordinator changes, and Mannion is a first-time play-caller.
- **Draft note:** In a 1QB, 4-pt-pass-TD league, QB is the cheapest position on the board; he's a fine target once the RB/WR/TE tiers thin, but there's no case for paying up given two straight QB8 seasons and a shrinking rush profile.
- **Confidence:** high
