## Alec Pierce — WR, IND, bye 13  (player_id: 8142)
- **Role:** starter — Colts' No. 1 receiver
- **Situation:** Re-signed in March on a four-year, $114M deal, then had surgery. Indianapolis traded Michael Pittman Jr. to Pittsburgh, vacating roughly 6.5 targets per game and moving Pierce and Josh Downs up the depth chart. Pierce is coming off a 1,003-yard season in which he led the NFL at 21.3 yards per catch, and both he and Daniel Jones have publicly talked about expanding his route tree beyond the pure field-stretcher role — Jones specifically praised his intermediate work on crossers, in-cuts and deep outs. Jones returns after a torn Achilles suffered in Week 14 of 2025; the Colts also kept Anthony Richardson and Riley Leonard. The target competition is Downs (slot), tight end Tyler Warren, and veteran signing Keenan Allen. Week 1 is Sept. 13 vs. Baltimore.
- **Health:** Cleared but under-prepared. He had surgery in late March on his left ankle/heel after a PRP injection failed to resolve an issue he'd played through in 2025, missed the entire offseason program and all of training camp on Active/PUP, and was activated off PUP on Aug. 26 — just ahead of the deadline that would have forced him to miss four games. Shane Steichen described the following days as a "ramp-up period." He last played a real game on Jan. 4. He made the 53-man roster and is eligible for the opener.
- **Risk:** Conditioning and rust more than the ankle itself. He hasn't practiced with the team since January and has roughly two weeks of ramp-up before the opener — a slow start, a snap count, or a soft-tissue injury from ramping too fast are all live. The deeper risk is the profile: he's a low-catch, high-aDOT receiver whose value is tied to a handful of deep completions per game, and that's a volatile week-to-week line even when healthy.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR a big-play, low-reception receiver loses less than he would in full PPR, and the yardage-per-catch profile plays fine at 0.1/yard. He's a WR3/FLEX with WR2 upside if the expanded route tree is real. The camp absence should have suppressed his price — that's the reason to take him, but treat Weeks 1-3 as uncertain.
- **Confidence:** medium-high (role is clear; his actual Week 1 workload after zero camp reps is not)
