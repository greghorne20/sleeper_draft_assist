## Davante Adams — WR, LAR, bye 11  (player_id: 2133)

- **Role:** starter (WR2 in the pecking order behind Puka Nacua, but the primary red-zone target)
- **Situation:** Second year in Los Angeles after finishing 2025 as the WR8 with 60 catches, 789 yards and a league-leading 14 TDs in 14 games. He ranked first among all receivers in end zone targets while sitting 18th in total targets — essentially the Rams' goal-line back. Weeks 1-14 he ran a 23.8% target share, 2.02 yards per route run, and led all WRs with 30 red zone targets. He still graded first in separation score and route win rate among 109 qualifying receivers. McVay explored trading him this offseason but he's back on his existing deal, which expires after this season. Stafford is entering his age-38 season. Nacua is the clear No. 1.
- **Health:** Healthy. He missed three games in 2025 with a hamstring issue that recurred in Week 16 but returned for the Wild Card round. In 2026 camp his absences were scheduled rest days (McVay confirmed), and he sat the preseason opener along with most Rams starters. Per ESPN, Nacua is dealing with psoas soreness a week out from the Sept. 10 opener vs. San Francisco, which could push more early-season volume to Adams.
- **Risk:** Touchdown regression. Roughly 49.3% of his fantasy points came on end zone targets last year against a league average of 14.5% for receivers, and the Rams made 76 red zone trips — the most by any team since the 2021 Bills — which is unlikely to repeat. Strip the TDs and the yardage floor is thin: he had one game over 75 receiving yards from Weeks 6-18. Age 33 (turning 34), and receivers 33+ averaging 16+ PPG since 2011 is a list of four names.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR the TD-heavy profile holds up better than in full PPR — 6-point receiving TDs carry more weight when receptions are worth half. Still, his WR22 ADP is baking in only mild regression. Reasonable WR2 if he slides past that; a fade if you're paying the fifth round.
- **Confidence:** high
