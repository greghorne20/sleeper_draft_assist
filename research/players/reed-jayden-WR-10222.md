## Jayden Reed — WR, GB, bye 11  (player_id: 10222)

- **Role:** starter, but role-limited — the designated slot receiver in three-WR sets, behind Christian Watson and Matthew Golden in two-receiver looks
- **Situation:** Green Bay signed Reed to a three-year, $50.25M extension ($20M guaranteed) in April, then cleared out the room around him — Dontayvion Wicks was traded to Philadelphia and Romeo Doubs left in free agency, vacating a meaningful chunk of targets that the Packers did not directly replace. Reports indicate the plan is Watson and Golden in 11-personnel with Reed added as the slot. The catch is that Green Bay ran three-WR sets on only 55.6% of snaps in 2025 (22nd), down from 67.3% in 2024. Reed's baseline in the last mostly healthy year: 11.6 PPG on a 63.7% snap share and 16.2% target share; he finished WR26 in 2023 and WR38 in 2024. TE Tucker Kraft is working back from a November ACL/partial LCL/partial meniscus injury, which could open early-season targets.
- **Health:** Healthy. He briefly missed practice with an ankle issue in camp and returned; the team said it isn't a long-term concern. 2025 was wrecked by a preseason foot injury and then a broken collarbone requiring surgery in September, limiting him to seven games.
- **Risk:** Snap share. If Green Bay stays in heavier personnel at 2025 rates, Reed is a 60%-snap slot receiver with a mid-teens target share, and that's a bench player in a 12-team league regardless of efficiency. The collarbone/foot history is a second strike — he's missed 10 games in a season already.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR this is a WR3/flex worth taking around WR40–45 (roughly rounds 8–10), and the argument for him is that his floor is already priced in — several outlets rank him a handful of spots ahead of ADP.
- **Confidence:** high
