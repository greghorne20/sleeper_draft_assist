## Jared Goff — QB, DET, bye 6  (player_id: 3163)

- **Role:** starter (entrenched)
- **Situation:** Goff is entering his 11th season and has topped 4,400 passing yards and 29 TDs in each of the last four; 2025 was 393-of-578, 4,564 yards, 34 TDs, 8 INTs, good for QB9 in total fantasy points. Since 2022 he leads the NFL in passing yards and passing touchdowns. The supporting cast is arguably the league's best — two returning 1,000-yard receivers, Sam LaPorta back at full health, Jahmyr Gibbs in a true bell-cow role. What changed this offseason is the offensive line: LT Taylor Decker and C/G Graham Glasgow are gone, with ex-Panthers C Cade Mays and first-round Clemson OT Blake Miller stepping in. Detroit dropped four of its final six games in 2025 and finished last in the NFC North despite scoring plenty.
- **Health:** No reported injury. He played all 17 games in 2025 and took a career-high 38 sacks.
- **Risk:** The floor is high and the ceiling is capped by the same thing every year — zero rushing production. He got a league-worst −1.9% of his fantasy points from rushing last season. In a 4-point-passing-TD, 0.04-per-yard format, that missing rushing floor matters more than it would in a 6-point-TD league, and games where Gibbs simply takes over on the ground quietly cost him volume. Rebuilt interior line with a non-mobile QB is the specific failure mode.
- **Draft note:** With one QB slot in a 12-teamer, Goff is exactly the "wait on QB" target — he's going around QB16 in ADP, which is roughly the 11th–13th round here. In this scoring he's a high-floor, low-ceiling QB1/QB2 border play, best if you stack him with a Lions pass catcher.
- **Confidence:** high
