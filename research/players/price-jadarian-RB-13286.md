## Jadarian Price — RB, SEA, bye 11  (player_id: 13286)

- **Role:** starter, provisionally — lead back for at least the first four games
- **Situation:** Rookie, the final pick of the first round (No. 32 overall) out of Notre Dame. Seattle lost Super Bowl MVP Kenneth Walker III to Kansas City in free agency, and Zach Charbonnet was placed on the PUP list, meaning he misses at least the first four games while rehabbing an ACL tear suffered in the postseason (reconstructive surgery in late February). That leaves Price atop the depth chart ahead of George Holani and Emanuel Wilson. The context is excellent: no team logged more RB carries or more red zone RB carries than Seattle last season, and the defending champs ranked third in overall rush rate. At Notre Dame he was stuck behind Jeremiyah Love (No. 3 overall this year) with only 280 career carries, but posted a 4.13 yards after contact (second best in the class) and a 3.6 forced missed tackle rate (third). He was not a receiver — 15 catches in 41 games. Seattle also lost OC Klint Kubiak.
- **Health:** Cleared, with a caveat. He dealt with lower-body soreness in early August, missed the preseason opener, and ultimately played zero preseason snaps. He was back taking most reps at practice by Aug. 17 and by early September ESPN's Brady Henderson reported he looked ready to be the lead guy.
- **Risk:** Charbonnet's return, which is baked into a Week 5-ish timeline. Charbonnet has 20 rushing TDs over the last two seasons and is a proven goal-line vulture, so Price could hold the job for a month and then lose the touchdowns that drive his value. Secondary risk: zero receiving profile means a 0.5 PPR floor that depends entirely on carries and scores, and rookies typically start slow — Price's slow-start window overlaps exactly with the only stretch he's guaranteed the job.
- **Draft note:** RB24-26 ADP (~pick 63) is fair. In this format his lack of receiving work costs him relative to committee backs who catch passes, but Seattle's run volume and red zone share are the best in the league. Draft him as an RB3 you start in September and be prepared to trade him before Charbonnet returns.
- **Confidence:** medium
