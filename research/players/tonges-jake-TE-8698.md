## Jake Tonges — TE, SF, bye 8  (player_id: 8698)
- **Role:** backup behind George Kittle, with a live path to starter snaps
- **Situation:** Tonges went from practice squad to real contributor in 2025 when Kittle got hurt in Week 1, finishing around 34-328 with multiple TDs and earning a two-year, $8M extension — the biggest deal of his career and a signal the 49ers want him involved. Kittle suffered an Achilles injury in the January 2026 playoffs and opened camp on the active/PUP list; he has since been activated and is expected to be ready for Week 1, but is still being monitored. Blocking TE Luke Farrell is behind them. Kyle Shanahan offense, Brock Purdy at QB, and the receiver room is thinned out (Kirk on IR, Pearsall on IR, Aiyuk gone).
- **Health:** He dealt with a plantar fascia injury in the January playoff loss to Seattle and is reported back to full health. No current designation.
- **Risk:** If Kittle is fine, Tonges is a special-teams tight end who barely plays — that's exactly what happened down the stretch in 2025, when he was listed third on the depth chart and logged zero offensive snaps in Week 18. The pick fails the moment Kittle stays healthy.
- **Draft note:** In a 1-TE, 12-team half-PPR league he isn't a draft pick — he's the best Kittle handcuff on the board and a priority waiver claim the week Kittle's Achilles flares. Tonges' fill-in rate last year (about 11.8 PPG, top-10 TE pace) is what makes him worth tracking.
- **Confidence:** medium
