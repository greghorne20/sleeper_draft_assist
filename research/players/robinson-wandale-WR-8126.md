## Wan'Dale Robinson — WR, TEN, bye 9  (player_id: 8126)

- **Role:** starter — slot/flex receiver, projected WR2 in the offense behind or alongside rookie Carnell Tate, with Calvin Ridley also in the room
- **Situation:** Tennessee signed Robinson to a four-year, $70M deal in free agency, reuniting him with Brian Daboll, now the Titans' OC, who oversaw his 2025 breakout in New York: 92 catches, 1,014 yards, 4 TDs on 140 targets in 16 games, with a 26.8%–29.6% target share (11th among qualifiers), a 32.9% first-read share, and an aDOT that rose to 8.7 — he led all receivers with 622 slot receiving yards. He's listed as capable of playing outside and in the slot. The competition is real this year with Tate drafted No. 4 overall and Ridley still on the roster, and one depth chart has Robinson behind Tate and Ridley in the base alignment.
- **Health:** Expected available for Week 1. He took a hard hit from Bears S Xavier Woods in a joint practice in mid-August and left early; Robert Saleh said he'd be evaluated for a concussion, subsequent tests apparently ruled one out, and the team continued monitoring symptoms with reports calling him on track for the opener. He dealt with a rib issue late in 2025 and has a prior ACL tear (2022).
- **Risk:** His role may narrow to three-receiver sets only, and his production is tied to Cam Ward, who was among the league's least effective passers as a rookie. He is a 5-foot-8, 185-pound volume receiver with almost no touchdown equity (9 receiving TDs in 54 career games) — if the target share drops from 27% toward 20% in a bad offense, he's a low-end flex. The concussion evaluation is also worth tracking through the Week 1 injury report.
- **Draft note:** A steady 0.5 PPR flex/WR3 around WR38–42 (roughly rounds 8–10). The reception volume plays every week regardless of how bad the Titans are, which is the whole case for him.
- **Confidence:** medium (Week 1 availability looks likely but the head-injury evaluation wasn't fully closed out in what I found, and the depth-chart order behind Tate/Ridley is disputed between sources)
