## Sam LaPorta — TE, DET, bye 6  (player_id: 10859)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** His 2025 ended after nine games with a herniated disc requiring back surgery, but he was productive before it — 40 catches for 489 yards and 3 TDs on 49 targets, a 17-game pace of roughly 76/924/6, TE7 in PPG and TE4 in three of his final four fully healthy games. Since his record-setting rookie year (86 catches, 889 yards, 10 TDs, TE1 overall) he's settled into the 10.9-11.9 PPG range. The 2026 hook is scheme: new offensive coordinator Drew Petzing used a second tight end at a higher rate than all but two current play callers in Arizona, where Trey McBride finished no worse than TE2 in PPG the last two years. LaPorta has been the league's highest-scoring fantasy TE per route out of multi-TE sets over the past two seasons, and The Athletic reported Petzing's scheme could make him an even bigger priority. Target competition is heavy: Amon-Ra St. Brown (172 targets), Jameson Williams and Jahmyr Gibbs.
- **Health:** Trending healthy but not without noise. He was limited during offseason workouts rehabbing the back surgery, then suffered a hip injury around Aug. 19-20 — Dan Campbell said he could be ready for the season but didn't know "for sure" — and missed multiple camp practices. He has since returned to practice and is reported on track to start the season fully healthy. Reporting on the back itself is uniformly positive; multiple outlets describe him as all systems go for Week 1 vs. New Orleans on Sept. 13.
- **Risk:** He is fourth in line for targets on his own team, and that's the structural problem no scheme change fully solves. The specific failure mode is a recurrence — a herniated disc plus a summer hip issue in the same calendar year is a real durability flag for a position that takes contact. If the back barks, this is a lost season, and unlike the target-share concern, that one has no floor.
- **Draft note:** TE5-ish by ADP but coming off the board a round or two after Tyler Warren and Colston Loveland, which is the argument for him in a 1-TE, no-premium league — you get most of the upside at a materially cheaper price. Reasonable target in the Round 7-8 range as a TE1 you didn't overpay for.
- **Confidence:** medium
