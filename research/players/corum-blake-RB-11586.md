## Blake Corum — RB, LAR, bye 11  (player_id: 11586)

- **Role:** committee / high-value backup — RB2 behind Kyren Williams, but treated as something closer to a co-starter
- **Situation:** Corum played all 17 games in 2025 and rushed 145 times for 746 yards and 6 TDs, adding just 8 catches for 36 yards on 14 targets. His snap share climbed through the year — under 22% over the first six games, then averaging 32.8% over the last seven — and he led the Rams in rushing in the NFC Championship Game when Williams struggled. Williams remains the listed starter after a 1,500-yard, 13-TD season. McVay held Corum out of the entire preseason along with the team's other key players, which reporting reads as a sign he's viewed as a co-starter rather than depth. The Rams also traded Jarquez Hunter to Miami for Tutu Atwell, thinning the room behind the top two.
- **Health:** No injury reported.
- **Risk:** Williams stays healthy and Corum stays at ~145 carries with essentially no receiving role. That's the base case, and it makes him a bye-week fill-in rather than a starter. His play style closely mirrors Williams', so there's no natural passing-down or change-of-pace carve-out to grow into — the eight catches last season are the whole story. He is worth substantially more the moment Williams misses time, and much less otherwise.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR the near-zero receiving usage really hurts — he's a touchdown-and-volume flier, not a flex you start confidently. He's being ranked around RB32, which several analysts flag as too rich for his likely workload. Better as a round 9–11 pick, and a near-must if you draft Kyren Williams.
- **Confidence:** high
