## Bijan Robinson — RB, ATL, bye 11  (player_id: 9509)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Undisputed lead back in Atlanta; Tyler Allgeier left for Arizona, leaving Brian Robinson Jr. as the primary backup. Signed a three-year extension worth up to $75M (Aug. 4, 2026), making him the NFL's highest-paid RB by total value; he led the NFL in scrimmage yards in 2025 (2,298) on 366 touches with 11 TDs. Kevin Stefanski is the new head coach and the QB job is unsettled.
- **Health:** Healthy. Did not play in the preseason (deliberate ramp-up after returning to practice); no injury designation entering Week 1.
- **Risk:** QB uncertainty suppressing red-zone and scoring efficiency, plus Brian Robinson Jr. siphoning goal-line work — volume is safe, but the offense has to function for the ceiling.
- **Draft note:** Top-3 overall; the ~79-catch receiving floor plays well at 0.5 PPR — worth 1.01–1.03.
- **Confidence:** high
