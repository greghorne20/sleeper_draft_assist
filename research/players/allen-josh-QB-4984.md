## Josh Allen — QB, BUF, bye 7  (player_id: 4984)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Franchise QB under new head coach Joe Brady (promoted from OC). He has started 127 consecutive regular-season games, the longest active streak in the NFL. DJ Moore was added this offseason; Khalil Shakir, Dalton Kincaid and James Cook remain the core weapons, though Shakir had an undisclosed early-September injury and was expected available for the opener vs. Houston.
- **Health:** Recovered from late-January 2026 surgery to remove a broken bone in his right foot; cleared for the offseason program and camp with no indication he'll miss time.
- **Risk:** At 4-point passing TDs his value leans on rushing scores — a dip in designed rushing volume or a foot re-aggravation would cut his edge over the QB field. The WR corps is also thinner than in peak years.
- **Draft note:** Top-3 QB, but 4-pt pass TDs compress the position — take him rounds 4–6, not earlier.
- **Confidence:** high
