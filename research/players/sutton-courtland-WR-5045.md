## Courtland Sutton — WR, DEN, bye 10  (player_id: 5045)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Sutton is coming off 74-1,017-7 (12.9 half-PPR PPG, WR24 in per-game scoring) as Bo Nix's unquestioned No. 1, but the offseason reordered the hierarchy: Sean Payton traded three 2026 picks for Jaylen Waddle, who is being drafted roughly 28 spots ahead of Sutton. Analysts across the board now describe it as a Waddle/Sutton one-two rather than Sutton as the alpha, with Troy Franklin and Pat Bryant behind them. Denver was 4th in pass attempts and pass rate over expectation in 2025, so there's volume to go around, but essentially no vacated targets — Waddle's share has to come from somewhere. Sutton is on the four-year deal he signed last summer and is now a pure outside receiver.
- **Health:** No current designation. He was limited early in training camp and was back in full pads by late July with no reported setbacks since.
- **Risk:** Efficiency was already thin under the surface. Among 109 qualifiers in 2025 he was 35th in target-per-route-run, 32nd in YPRR, and 29th in first downs per route run — that was as the clear WR1. He turns 31 in October and his TD rate (11.7% over three years) is doing a lot of the work in his 2025 line. Dropping to the second rung while relying on touchdown variance is the specific way this pick fails.
- **Draft note:** In half-PPR he's a high-end flex / low-end WR2 rather than a WR2 you build around. ADP is right around pick 77 (Round 7); that's fair value, but taking him ahead of Waddle or before the mid-6th is where you get hurt.
- **Confidence:** high
