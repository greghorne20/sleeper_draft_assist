## Carnell Tate — WR, TEN, bye 9  (player_id: 13279)

- **Role:** starter (rookie WR1 / primary outside receiver)
- **Situation:** Tate went No. 4 overall out of Ohio State in the 2026 draft and signed a four-year, $51.1M deal. He is the only Titans rookie listed with the first team on the team's unofficial depth chart and is expected to be Cam Ward's top downfield weapon, working alongside slot man Wan'Dale Robinson with Calvin Ridley still in the mix. New OC Brian Daboll is expected to push this offense toward the pass to fit Ward. Tate was efficient rather than high-volume in college (121-1,872-14 over three years; 13.1 yards per target and 3.06 yards per route run in 2025, first and third in his class).
- **Health:** No significant injury. He missed a practice in early September with what Robert Saleh described as "stiffness" (undisclosed); no reporting suggests his Week 1 status against the Jets is at risk.
- **Risk:** The environment, not the role. The Titans' offensive line projects among the league's worst, Ward was sacked 55 times last year, and Tennessee is widely projected to be one of the lower-scoring offenses in the league — which caps the target-volume-to-points conversion even if the target share is real. He also went 0-for-6 on targets across 38 preseason snaps in three exhibition games, and rookie WRs rarely produce every week. Ridley and Robinson are live competition for looks.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR he's a WR3 with WR2 upside, in the same tier one analyst grouped with Mike Evans and Christian Watson — around the fifth-to-sixth round in a 12-team draft. The price is unusually reasonable for a No. 4 overall receiver, which is most of the case for him.
- **Confidence:** high (on role); medium on production, given the offense
