## Chase Brown — RB, CIN, bye 6  (player_id: 9224)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Brown is Cincinnati's lead back for a third straight year and the offense is being built further around him. He posted 232 carries for 1,019 yards and 6 TDs plus a career-best 69 catches for 437 yards and 5 TDs in 2025, finishing 8th among RBs in PPR points per game across all 17 games. Camp reporting (Paul Dehner Jr., The Athletic) has OC Dan Pitcher moving him all over the formation — slot, jet sweep, 20 personnel, behind a fullback — and expects a heavier under-center run game. All five offensive line starters return. Samaje Perine remains the veteran complement; sixth-rounder Tahj Brooks is a threat to Perine's No. 2 job, not Brown's.
- **Health:** No injury reported. He played all 17 games last season.
- **Risk:** The Perine split plus Burrow's availability. Brown's snap share was only 63% when Perine was healthy, and he averaged 17.6 PPR PPG in the three games Perine was limited or out vs. 16.3 with him. He also ran hot on touchdowns — 8 scores in his final 5 games. And his ceiling is tethered to Joe Burrow: he averaged a much higher PPG with Burrow on the field than without.
- **Draft note:** Round 2 in your scoring, and arguably the best value in that range — 69 catches at 0.5 PPR is ~35 points of built-in floor that a pure early-down back doesn't have.
- **Confidence:** high
