## Trey McBride — TE, ARI, bye 14  (player_id: 8130)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Unquestioned TE1 in Arizona and the clear favorite to lead the team in targets, per Fox Sports' camp reporting, though the writeup notes he has more competition for the ball this year. Big offseason reset: Kyler Murray was released in March, Mike LaFleur is the new head coach, and Jacoby Brissett is the Week 1 starter with Gardner Minshew and rookie Carson Beck behind him. Arizona also added RBs Jeremiyah Love and Tyler Allgeier, which should improve a rushing attack that was second-worst in the league last year — that helps the offense but may reduce the excess target volume McBride absorbed. ESPN notes McBride led all TEs in every fantasy-relevant counting stat in 2025 and produced far better with Brissett (21.1 PPG for the offense) than with Murray (12.5).
- **Health:** No current injury. He was limited to one snap in the preseason opener vs. Las Vegas, which was rest/protection rather than injury. Draft Sharks and Fantasy Football Calculator both list no recent injuries.
- **Risk:** A LaFleur offense with a functional run game and more mouths (Marvin Harrison Jr., Michael Wilson, Kendrick Bourne) throws fewer desperation targets his way. The bigger tail risk is Brissett losing the job — LaFleur has been non-committal, and a Beck or Minshew takeover in a rebuilding offense caps the whole passing game.
- **Draft note:** In half-PPR with one TE slot and no TE premium, he's still a legitimate positional-advantage pick; ESPN puts him on the radar at the first/second-round turn, which feels like the right neighborhood in a 12-teamer.
- **Confidence:** high
