## James Cook — RB, BUF, bye 7  (player_id: 8138)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Buffalo's lead back with back-to-back 1,000-yard seasons and a league-leading 16 rushing TDs in 2024, now on a four-year, roughly $46–48M extension. He staged a brief hold-in during 2026 camp (stopped practicing Aug. 3 citing "business") and dressed but didn't play in the preseason opener. Frank Gore Jr. and Ty Johnson provide the depth behind him.
- **Health:** No injury reported; the camp absence was contractual. Assume healthy for the opener vs. Houston.
- **Risk:** Josh Allen vulturing goal-line carries and a committee feel near the end zone — his TD rate is the whole case for him, and at 0.5 PPR his ~30-catch receiving work doesn't cushion a TD regression.
- **Draft note:** RB2 in the Round 2–3 range, roughly picks 18–30; don't pay 2024's TD spike.
- **Confidence:** high
