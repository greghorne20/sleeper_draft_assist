## Kyle Monangai — RB, CHI, bye 10  (player_id: 12534)

- **Role:** committee (RB2 behind D'Andre Swift) — currently injured, Week 1 availability in doubt
- **Situation:** Monangai was a 2025 seventh-rounder who climbed to the RB2 job and finished with 169 carries for 783 yards and 5 TDs plus 18 catches for 164 yards in 17 games. From Week 7 on he split work with Swift regularly, and in Weeks 10–18 he actually out-carried Swift in the red zone 19–18. Nothing changed on the depth chart this offseason — Swift is still the starter in the final year of his deal — but Monangai hyperextended his right knee in an 11-on-11 session on Aug. 16 and has been week-to-week since.
- **Health:** Hyperextended right knee (Aug. 16). Initial tests showed the joint was structurally sound and Schefter reported early optimism, but an MRI pointed to multiple weeks. Ben Johnson reaffirmed "week-to-week" on Sept. 1 and Monangai still was not practicing. He was kept on the initial 53 and NOT placed on IR, which signals the Bears expect him back inside the first four weeks. He has progressed to running per Swift. Week 1 (Sept. 13 at Carolina) is genuinely in doubt; if he sits, Roschon Johnson takes change-of-pace duties.
- **Risk:** The knee lingers, he loses the ramp-up, and Roschon Johnson or a healthy Swift absorbs the committee share he built last year. Secondary risk: his profile is nearly all early-down power — 4.6 forty, a 5.1% college target share, only 30 targets as a rookie — so in a half-PPR format his floor depends almost entirely on carries and goal-line work, not receptions. Seventh-round backs also have a poor track record of holding gains in year two.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR he's an RB3/flex with elite-handcuff appeal rather than a weekly starter; the knee should push him past where the industry had him (RotoBaller RB40–42, ADP falling on the injury). Reasonable as a bench swing in the 9th–11th round range of a 13-round draft, and materially more valuable to whoever ends up with Swift.
- **Confidence:** medium (role is clear, but the injury timeline is unresolved as of Sept. 5)
