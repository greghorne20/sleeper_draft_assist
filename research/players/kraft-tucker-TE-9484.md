## Tucker Kraft — TE, GB, bye 11  (player_id: 9484)

- **Role:** starter (clear TE1), likely on a snap count in Week 1
- **Situation:** Kraft tore his right ACL in Week 9 (Nov. 2) of 2025 while ranked the overall TE1 in PPR. Through eight games he had 32 catches for 489 yards and 6 TDs, on pace for 1,000+ yards and 13 scores; his 15.3 yards per catch would have led all tight ends over a full season, and his 344 yards after contact still led all Packers receivers despite the missed time. Green Bay opened camp with him on PUP, activated him July 31, cleared him for full contact in late August, and he took hard hits in a joint practice against Arizona without issue. He'll sit the preseason finale but is expected to be full-go for Week 1 at Minnesota on Sept. 13. Around him, Doubs and Wicks are gone (131 targets vacated), Jonnu Smith was signed as depth with Luke Musgrave on reserve/PUP for at least four games, and Josh Jacobs starts on the commissioner's exempt list — all of which points more volume his way.
- **Health:** Roughly 10 months post-ACL. Cleared for full contact and expected to play Week 1. Reports suggest the Packers, historically conservative, may hold him to a snap count early; one analysis projects full-go by Week 2 or 3. Kraft himself has said he hopes to avoid a count. He is also unsigned on an extension, though the team wants it done before the opener — a holdout has not been threatened.
- **Risk:** The specific failure is a slow ramp — a Week 1–3 snap count that costs you three usable weeks, or a knee that isn't the same explosive player at the catch point. Green Bay's conservatism with ACL returns is well documented (Watson's own return took 294 days). Secondary risk: LaFleur's rotating target hierarchy and a run-first identity if Jacobs comes back.
- **Draft note:** In a one-TE, 0.5 PPR league, a healthy Kraft is a top-five tight end at a discount — he's being ranked around TE5 and treated as a late-round steal. That is exactly the kind of positional edge worth taking in rounds 5–7, with the understanding that the first two or three weeks may be quiet.
- **Confidence:** high
