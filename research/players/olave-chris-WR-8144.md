## Chris Olave — WR, NO, bye 8  (player_id: 8144)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** New Orleans' WR1, listed atop the post-cuts depth chart with Devaughn Vele second and rookie Barion Brown third. He set career highs in 2025 with 100 catches, 1,163 yards and nine TDs — sixth among WRs in half-PPR — on a 27.2% target share, seventh-best air-yards share (41.7%) and a fourth-ranked 30.2% red-zone target share. Kellen Moore returns for year two and moved him into the slot on a career-high 41.9% of routes. Tyler Shough is the clear starting QB with Spencer Rattler backing up. The key roster note: eighth-overall rookie Jordyn Tyson, drafted to be the complement, is on IR-R and expected to miss roughly two months, so the target competition Olave was projected to face isn't there early.
- **Health:** Expected to be available. He missed nine games in 2024 with two concussions and missed the 2025 finale after doctors found a blood clot in his lung — the concussion history in particular is a standing concern worth monitoring rather than an active injury.
- **Risk:** The concussion history is the specific thing that breaks this pick; two in one season already cost him more than half a year. Beyond that, a Saints offense that went 6-11 with a second-year QB has a low team-scoring ceiling, and Shough is not yet a proven starter.
- **Draft note:** Elite target and red-zone share in an offense with almost no competition for the ball right now; a WR13-ish ADP in the third round is fair value in half-PPR.
- **Confidence:** high
