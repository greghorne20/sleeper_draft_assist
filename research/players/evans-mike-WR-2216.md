## Mike Evans — WR, SF, bye 8  (player_id: 2216)

- **Role:** starter (San Francisco's nominal No. 1 receiver)
- **Situation:** Left Tampa Bay for the first time in his career, signing a three-year, $42.4M deal with the 49ers in March. John Lynch has spoken glowingly about the fit — a big-bodied receiver who can run the full route tree is something Shanahan hasn't had. The room around him is thin and shuffled: Ricky Pearsall is out for the season (PCL surgery), Deebo Samuel returned, Christian Kirk signed a one-year deal and profiles as the No. 3, rookie second-rounder De'Zhaun Stribling has flashed in camp, and George Kittle is working back from a torn Achilles. Brock Purdy is back from his own injury-wrecked year. 2025 was Evans' worst: 30 catches on 62 targets for 368 yards and 3 TDs in eight games, ending an 11-year streak of 1,000-yard seasons, with a hamstring injury, a clavicle fracture and a concussion. San Francisco opens vs. the Rams in Melbourne on Sept. 10.
- **Health:** The central issue, but trending up. He missed large stretches of camp — first a quad issue, then groin tightness, then an adductor problem, with the timeline slipping twice (Shanahan targeted the week of Aug. 31, then Lynch pushed it to the following week). ESPN's Jeremy Fowler reported Aug. 26 that Evans planned to play Week 1 and that the groin was considered minor. He returned to practice Wednesday, Sept. 2 and was reported to be moving well. No confirmed aggravation.
- **Risk:** Age 33 with a hamstring/soft-tissue pattern that has recurred in some form every year since 2019, and he's already burned most of camp — meaning almost no on-field chemistry built with Purdy. The specific failure: he opens the season limited or re-tweaks the adductor, Stribling and Deebo absorb the targets, and by the time he's right the offense has moved on without him. He was durable for 11 seasons before last year, so this isn't a long-standing pattern, but it's the live one.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR his historic strength — red zone size and TD volume — carries more weight than a reception-heavy profile would. That's the case for him. But this is a boom-or-bust pick: worth a Round 6-8 shot as a WR3 with WR2 upside, not a price you pay expecting weekly starts.
- **Confidence:** medium
