## Michael Wilson — WR, ARI, bye 14  (player_id: 10232)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Broke out in 2025 with 78-1,006-7, finishing WR10 in full PPR and WR2 from Weeks 11–18 after Jacoby Brissett took over at QB. The splits are the whole story: with Marvin Harrison Jr. on the field he averaged 7.6 PPR points on 4.8 targets across 12 games; without him, 25.9 points on 13.6 targets. New HC/playcaller Mike LaFleur has publicly compared his intended role to Puka Nacua's in Los Angeles, and Arizona backed the belief with a three-year, $75M extension ($47M guaranteed) — real money for a player who was on a rookie deal. Brissett returns as the Week 1 starter; Trey McBride remains the team's top target; the Cardinals threw on 63.9% of snaps last season but drafted RB Jeremiyah Love No. 3 overall, which may pull that number down.
- **Health:** No injury designation reported. He and Harrison have both been sharing passing-down work in preseason and both looked healthy.
- **Risk:** The production was manufactured by circumstance. He ran the second-most routes in the entire NFL last year — a volume that's very hard to repeat with a healthy Harrison and a rookie back soaking up early-down snaps. Add Brissett's career 6.6 YPA and rookie Carson Beck lurking, and the downside is a 5-target WR4 in a rebuilding offense. The contract argues the team believes in him; it doesn't guarantee targets.
- **Draft note:** Round 7–8 in half-PPR as a WR3/flex. He's the Arizona receiver I'd rather have than Harrison at similar cost, but don't pay the WR2 price his 2025 finish implies.
- **Confidence:** medium
