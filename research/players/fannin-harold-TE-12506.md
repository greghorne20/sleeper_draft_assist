## Harold Fannin — TE, CLE, bye 11  (player_id: 12506)

- **Role:** starter (unquestioned TE1 in Cleveland)
- **Situation:** Fannin caught 72 passes for 731 yards and 6 TDs on 107–108 targets as a 21-year-old rookie in 2025, playing 73.3% of snaps and finishing TE6 in PPR — and he did that while splitting the room with David Njoku and cycling through a quarterback carousel. Njoku signed with the Chargers in free agency, leaving Fannin the entire tight end room. Cleveland also changed head coaches, with Todd Monken taking over. The counterweight: the Browns spent two top-40 picks on WRs KC Concepcion and Denzel Boston, adding real target competition alongside Jerry Jeudy. Monken named Deshaun Watson the Week 1 starter over Shedeur Sanders, with Sanders as the No. 2 and Dillon Gabriel on IR-designated-to-return with a back injury.
- **Health:** No injury reported. He played 16 games with 13 starts as a rookie.
- **Risk:** Quarterback play. Both realistic options under center are underwhelming, and Watson has an extensive injury history plus a short leash — Cleveland opens with two difficult road games. A slow start could mean a mid-season QB change and a scheme reset. The other risk is that the rookie receivers, not Fannin, absorb Njoku's vacated targets, leaving him at a similar volume for a higher draft price. He's also still a weak in-line blocker, which could cap snaps in certain packages.
- **Draft note:** With one TE slot and 0.5 PPR, a 70–85 catch tight end is a real weekly edge. His ADP has already caught up (roughly 71–81, i.e. the sixth or seventh round, TE8 off the board), so you're paying for the projection rather than buying a discount. Fine as a set-and-forget TE1 in that range; don't reach past it.
- **Confidence:** high (role); medium on the scoring environment
