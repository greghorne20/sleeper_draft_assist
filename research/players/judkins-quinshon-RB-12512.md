## Quinshon Judkins — RB, CLE, bye 11  (player_id: 12512)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Listed as Cleveland's top back ahead of Dylan Sampson and Raheim Sanders, and the Browns expect him to lead a backfield that also includes Ahmani Marshall. Big coaching change: Todd Monken is now head coach with Travis Switzer at OC — Monken's Baltimore offenses ran more than they passed in all three of his years there, and Derrick Henry got 325 and 307 carries under him. The offensive line was rebuilt this offseason, which matters because Judkins had the fifth-highest share of yards coming after first contact among 49 qualifying backs as a rookie. Sampson projects as the passing-down back, capping his receiving work.
- **Health:** Recovered from the dislocated right ankle and fractured fibula suffered Week 16 of 2025 (Dec. 21 vs. Buffalo, surgery followed). He returned to team drills at minicamp, was full-go at the start of camp, and played in the preseason opener. In mid-August he missed two practices with an undisclosed issue Monken called "minor" and precautionary; he was back in team drills the following Tuesday. Expected fine for the Week 1 opener vs. Jacksonville on Sept. 13.
- **Risk:** The offense. Deshaun Watson is the quarterback, at least to start, with Shedeur Sanders and rookie Taylen Green behind him — a unit that may not generate enough scoring chances for a volume-dependent back. Judkins also averaged just 3.6 YPC as a rookie and ranked 40th of 49 in explosive run rate, so if the rebuilt line doesn't fix the efficiency, he's a high-touch back with a capped ceiling. The leg injury is a secondary concern, not the primary one.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR he's a volume RB2 without much reception equity (26 catches last year) — worth taking around the RB20-24 range where his ADP sits, but not a reach. Priced about right; the upside case needs the Browns' offense to improve, not just Judkins.
- **Confidence:** medium
