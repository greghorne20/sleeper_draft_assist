## Tre' Harris — WR, LAC, bye 7  (player_id: 12509)
- **Role:** starter-adjacent (projected WR3, on the field in three-receiver sets)
- **Situation:** 2025 second-rounder (No. 55) who played 49% of the Chargers' offensive snaps as a rookie but drew only an 8% target share, much of it spent blocking (30-324-1). Keenan Allen left for Indianapolis, clearing the No. 3 job. Jim Harbaugh said at OTAs that Harris is "clearly" part of the top three with Ladd McConkey and Quentin Johnston, and reporting has Week 1 shaping up as those three. Mike McDaniel is the new playcaller, which should mean a more pass-friendly scheme; Justin Herbert at QB. Competition is KeAndre Lambert-Smith and rookie Brenen Thompson.
- **Health:** He sat out the preseason game against San Francisco with an undisclosed issue and did not play in the finale vs. the Rams, but John Harbaugh said he'd practice that weekend and it was characterized as minor. Some outlets carried a Week 1 return estimate of 9/13. Not a clean bill of health, but no reported structural injury. Exact nature: UNKNOWN.
- **Risk:** He was WR4 as a rookie because he wasn't productive, not only because of the depth chart — 8% target share is a real red flag, and the No. 3 job in an offense that funnels to McConkey may not be worth much. The late-preseason undisclosed injury is an unresolved question days from Week 1.
- **Draft note:** Best late-round upside swing among the receivers in this batch. In a 13-round half-PPR league he's a final-pick bench stash: if McDaniel's scheme lifts the passing volume and Johnston stumbles, there's WR3 upside; if not, he's droppable by Week 4.
- **Confidence:** medium
