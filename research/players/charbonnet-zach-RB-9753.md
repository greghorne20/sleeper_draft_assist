## Zach Charbonnet — RB, SEA, bye 11  (player_id: 9753)

- **Role:** UNKNOWN for the first quarter of the season — he is on reserve/PUP and will not play. Role when he returns is unsettled.
- **Situation:** Charbonnet tore the ACL in his left knee in Seattle's Jan. 17 divisional-round win over San Francisco and had surgery Feb. 20. Seattle moved him from active/PUP to reserve/PUP at the Aug. 30 roster deadline, which locks in a minimum four-game absence — earliest possible return is Week 5 (Oct. 11 vs. SF), and Seattle has not named a target date. Several analysts read the timeline (9–10 months post-injury) as pointing to Week 7 or after the Week 11 bye. In the meantime the backfield belongs to first-round rookie Jadarian Price, George Holani and free-agent Emanuel Wilson. Kenneth Walker III left for Kansas City. Charbonnet ran for 730 yards and 12 TDs on 148–184 carries in 16 games in 2025 (sources differ on the carry total) and led the position group in carries inside the 5.
- **Health:** On reserve/PUP recovering from a January ACL tear (Feb. 20 surgery). Out at least four games. Rotowire characterized the move as procedural and said he remains "far away" from returning.
- **Risk:** The obvious one is that Price seizes the job while Charbonnet is out and never gives it back — Seattle spent a first-round pick on him. Even in the good case, Charbonnet's receiving usage is minimal (career-low 24 targets in 2025), which caps him in a 0.5 PPR format, and he's never finished as a top-20 back. You may be paying for a goal-line specialist who debuts in Week 7 on a snap count.
- **Draft note:** A late-round stash only — his ADP sits around 124, and with six bench spots in a 13-round draft, he's a reasonable last-two-rounds lottery ticket if you're already deep at RB. Not a pick you make expecting starts before November.
- **Confidence:** high (on the injury and PUP status); medium on what his role looks like on return
