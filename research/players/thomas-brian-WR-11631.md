## Brian Thomas — WR, JAX, bye 7  (player_id: 11631)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** (Rostered as Brian Thomas Jr.) One of 2025's biggest fantasy busts — 48-707-2 on 91 targets in 14 games, 8.2 PPG down from 14.1 as a rookie — after a high ankle sprain and a shoulder issue. Once Jakobi Meyers arrived at the deadline, Thomas was reduced to a field-stretcher: 14.9% target share on an 18.1 aDOT over Weeks 13–18. The 2026 case is coaching-driven. Liam Coen has praised his offseason work repeatedly ("a different dude in terms of confidence," route-running "unreal") and anticipates a major downfield role, and Jacksonville's own reporting documented better timing with Trevor Lawrence and multiple deep TDs in open practices. Countervailing signal: the Jaguars were reportedly entertaining trade offers for him this offseason, and Parker Washington has overshadowed him in camp coverage.
- **Health:** Shoulder. He landed awkwardly on it during a joint practice with Tampa Bay in late August, avoided serious injury, sat out the preseason finale, and was a full participant in practice by Sept. 1. No Week 1 concern per Coen.
- **Risk:** The role, not the talent. A deep-only usage pattern in a four-mouth passing game (Washington, Meyers, Brenton Strange, plus whatever offensive snaps Travis Hunter gets) produces enormous week-to-week volatility — two top-24 weeks in his final six games last year. If the target share stays in the mid-teens, he's a boom/bust flex regardless of how good he looks.
- **Draft note:** In half-PPR he's a Round 5–6 upside swing on your bench-to-flex, priced as a WR3. The rookie-year ceiling is real but you're buying variance, not a floor.
- **Confidence:** medium
