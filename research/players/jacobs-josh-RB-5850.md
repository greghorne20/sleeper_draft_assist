## Josh Jacobs — RB, GB, bye 11  (player_id: 5850)

- **Role:** UNAVAILABLE — on the NFL's Commissioner's Exempt List, not on the 53-man roster
- **Situation:** When available, Jacobs is Green Bay's unquestioned bell cow: 929 rush yards and 14 total TDs in 15 games in 2025, top-13 in RB PPR PPG in five of the last six seasons, and 28 rushing TDs over the past two years. His top 2025 touch competition, Emanuel Wilson, signed with Seattle. But on Aug. 30 the NFL placed him on the Commissioner's Exempt List after he was formally charged with misdemeanor battery and criminal damage to property stemming from a May 26 arrest (originally booked on five counts including felony strangulation). He cannot practice, play, or attend games; he continues to be paid. GM Brian Gutekunst said he expects Jacobs to play for the team in 2026 but declined to rule out a release. Green Bay traded a 2028 sixth to Pittsburgh for Kaleb Johnson and also has MarShawn Lloyd and Chris Brooks.
- **Health:** He dealt with a groin injury during camp but otherwise participated fully. He missed two games in 2025 with a lingering left knee contusion. His unavailability right now is disciplinary, not medical.
- **Risk:** There is no timetable. His first court appearance isn't until Nov. 17, and the league's investigation is separate from the criminal case, so he could be reinstated before then or suspended at any point. Time on the exempt list counts toward a later suspension. He could also be released. This is not a normal "misses four games" situation — the range of outcomes runs from Week 3 return to the whole season.
- **Draft note:** Not a starting-roster pick. In a 13-round league with six bench spots, he's a late-round stash only if you can afford to hold a roster spot with no return date — and if you take him, MarShawn Lloyd or Kaleb Johnson is the pick that actually matters.
- **Confidence:** high on the facts of his status; low on any projection of games played.
