## Tony Pollard — RB, TEN, bye 9  (player_id: 5967)

- **Role:** starter, but in a committee — 1A to Tyjae Spears' 1B
- **Situation:** Pollard started all 17 games in 2025 and remains atop the depth chart. Tennessee was heavily mocked to take Jeremiyah Love at No. 4 and instead took Carnell Tate, which preserved Pollard's job; the only backfield addition was fifth-rounder Nicholas Singleton. He punched in the opening-drive TD in the preseason opener against San Francisco, which reads as confirmation of the starting role after an offseason in which he was frequently listed as a cut candidate. He's chasing a fifth straight 1,000-yard season and is in the final year of the three-year deal he signed in March 2024. New OC Brian Daboll is expected to lean more pass-heavy around Cam Ward, Wan'Dale Robinson and Tate.
- **Health:** No current injury reported. He missed a practice in mid-August alongside Singleton and Kalel Mullings; nothing since indicates a lingering problem. UNKNOWN whether that was anything beyond routine.
- **Risk:** Efficiency and pass-game share. Analysts describe him as close to a dirt-average back on a per-play basis, and Spears has avoided more tackles per touch than any back in the league over the last three seasons (33.4% on 300+ touches) while handling passing downs. A Daboll offense that throws more shrinks the early-down carry pie Pollard depends on, and Spears is the direct beneficiary. Singleton is a third body who could eat goal-line work.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR, Pollard is a volume-dependent RB3/flex, not an RB2 — he needs 15+ carries and TDs to pay off because Spears takes the receptions. Fair around rounds 7–9 in a 12-team, 13-round draft; the Spears half of this backfield may be the better buy at cost.
- **Confidence:** medium (role is confirmed; workload split under a brand-new staff is not)
