## Brian Robinson — RB, ATL, bye 11  (player_id: 8154)

- **Role:** backup (with a defined short-yardage/goal-line package)
- **Situation:** (Rostered as Brian Robinson Jr.) Signed a one-year, $2.5M deal with Atlanta in March to replace Tyler Allgeier, who left for Arizona. He's the clear No. 2 behind Bijan Robinson under new HC Kevin Stefanski, and the interesting part is the goal-line role: multiple reports out of camp say he's "cemented his role as the new Tyler Allgeier" with a physical practice style and is expected to handle primary goal-line work. That matters because Allgeier out-carried Bijan 19-to-17 inside the 10 last season and matched him at seven carries inside the five, converting four of them. At 6-1, 225 he fits the profile. He spent 2025 backing up Christian McCaffrey in San Francisco (100 touches, 425 scrimmage yards) after three straight 700+ yard rushing seasons in Washington.
- **Health:** No injury designation found.
- **Risk:** Bijan Robinson stays healthy and Stefanski uses him as a true workhorse — Stefanski gave Nick Chubb 16.7 carries a game in Cleveland and Quinshon Judkins 16.4 last year, so the "complementary" back may end up with well under 10 touches. Brian is also a non-factor as a receiver (92 career targets), so there's no PPR floor to fall back on. That's a touchdown-or-bust week most weeks.
- **Draft note:** In half-PPR, last-pick territory — Round 12–13 as a bench stash. The goal-line role gives him more standalone value than a pure handcuff, but you're mostly buying the Bijan insurance, which is worth a lot if you own Bijan and much less if you don't.
- **Confidence:** high
