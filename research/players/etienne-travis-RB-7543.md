## Travis Etienne — RB, NO, bye 8  (player_id: 7543)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Signed a four-year, roughly $52M deal with New Orleans in March and is the lead back, listed atop the Saints' depth chart. He's coming off an RB13 finish in points per game with Jacksonville — 260 carries for 1,107 yards, 296 total touches, 1,399 total yards and a career-high 13 TDs, fifth in the NFL in red-zone touches. In camp he took most of the early-down reps while Alvin Kamara handled third downs, an arrangement Jeff Duncan of Nola.com expects to carry into Week 1. OC Doug Nussmeier has publicly called him elite. He rested all three preseason games and finished camp healthy.
- **Health:** Fully healthy entering Week 1 at Detroit; he was one of the few Saints backs to avoid a camp setback. Kamara is out about a month with a sprained MCL, and Devin Neal (hamstring) and Ty Chandler (knee) are on IR for the season — Kendre Miller and Audric Estime are the remaining depth, both banged up in camp.
- **Risk:** He is volume-dependent without special efficiency — 37th in explosive run rate, 31st in missed tackle rate, 34th in yards after contact per attempt last year. Once Kamara returns, the receiving work goes back to a committee, and the Saints' RB target share was only 13% last season versus Jacksonville's 17%. A 6-11 team with a second-year QB also limits scoring opportunities.
- **Draft note:** The Kamara MCL sprain hands him a near-workhorse role for the opening month, which is real value in a redraft league; a fair RB2 around the RB15–18 range in half-PPR, where the reduced receiving share hurts less than it would in full PPR.
- **Confidence:** high
