## Brock Purdy — QB, SF, bye 8  (player_id: 8183)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Signed a five-year, $265M extension before 2025, then lost most of the year to injury — he suited up for nine games, throwing for 2,167 yards, 20 TDs and completing 69.4% while Mac Jones went 5-3 in his place. When he did play he averaged a career-best 24.2 fantasy PPG with at least 20.7 in seven of 11 games including the playoffs, and he's averaged 20.5+ PPG in each of the last three seasons. The supporting cast turned over hard: Mike Evans signed a three-year, $42.4M deal, Deebo Samuel returned, Christian Kirk came in on a one-year deal and looks like the No. 3, rookie second-rounder De'Zhaun Stribling has flashed (a 50-yard camp TD), and Ricky Pearsall is out for the season after PCL surgery. George Kittle is coming back from a torn Achilles and is a question mark for Week 1. Kyle Shanahan is still the play caller. San Francisco opens vs. the Rams in Melbourne on Sept. 10.
- **Health:** Expected to be fine. His 2025 was wrecked by a shoulder injury and turf toe suffered in Week 1 vs. Seattle; he returned briefly in Week 4, sat again, and came back late in the year. He was reported to have had one of his better camp practices in mid-August with no restrictions noted.
- **Risk:** Two things. First, his own durability — he's now missed eight games in a season and the toe injury lingered. Second, and more specific: this passing game is held together with tape. Pearsall is gone for the year, Evans is 33 and has spent most of camp on the side with quad/groin/adductor issues, Kittle is returning from an Achilles, and the No. 1 receiver may end up being a rookie. Purdy's fantasy value has never come from volume, so a diminished cast plus an elite defense that shortens games caps him.
- **Draft note:** With 4-point pass TDs and 25 yards per passing point, this is a QB-suppressing format — wait on the position. Purdy at a QB12-13 price (roughly Round 7-8) is fair value in a 1QB, 13-round league; if he slides to Round 9 he's a clear buy. Don't reach.
- **Confidence:** medium
