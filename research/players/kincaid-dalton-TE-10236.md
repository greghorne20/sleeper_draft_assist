## Dalton Kincaid — TE, BUF, bye 7  (player_id: 10236)

- **Role:** starter (with a real snap-share question)
- **Situation:** Buffalo picked up his fifth-year option in March. On a per-route basis he was the best tight end in football in 2025 — among 54 qualifiers he ranked first in target-per-route-run, YPRR, separation score, route win rate, first downs per route run, and fantasy points per route run — and still only finished TE13 in PPG (39-571-5 in 12 games, 8.8 half-PPR PPG). The reason is playing time: 44.2% route share, 39th at the position. GM Brandon Beane said in February that the limited usage was partly health-driven and that the goal is to keep him on the field more. The Athletic's Joe Buscaglia reported he's practiced every day this camp, looks stronger, is blocking with more physicality, and has seen a usage bump even with Dawson Knox and Jackson Hawes healthy. Joe Brady is now in full control of the offense as head coach, with Josh Allen throwing and DJ Moore added to the receiver room.
- **Health:** Healthy now, and notably has not missed a workout — which is the news, because he reportedly played through a torn PCL in 2025 and managed it across two seasons. He's missed nine games in two years to hamstring and knee issues; surgery was not required this offseason.
- **Risk:** The route share never climbs. If he stays in the 45–55% range because Buffalo keeps rotating tight ends and leans run-heavy, he's a low-end TE2 with great rate stats and no volume — exactly what he was last year. The injury history is the mechanism by which that happens.
- **Draft note:** His ADP has fallen from around pick 107 in late July toward the 13th round, and at that price he's the best cheap TE swing on the board in half-PPR. Draft him as your TE2 in a two-TE build, or as a TE1 you're willing to bail on by Week 3 if the Week 1 snap count comes back ugly.
- **Confidence:** medium
