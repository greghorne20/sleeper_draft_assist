## Keenan Allen — WR, IND, bye 13  (player_id: 1479)
- **Role:** starter (projected to start opposite Alec Pierce and Josh Downs), with legal risk attached
- **Situation:** Signed a one-year deal with the Colts on Aug 19, 2026, reuniting with Shane Steichen. Indianapolis traded Michael Pittman Jr. to Pittsburgh and was thin behind Pierce (who spent camp on PUP with an ankle issue before being activated) and Downs. Allen caught 81 passes for 777 yards and 4 TDs with the Chargers in 2025 and graded well in route-win rate; he profiles as the Pittman replacement, working the perimeter and slot. Daniel Jones is the starting QB, Tyler Warren is a heavy-usage TE.
- **Health:** No physical injury reported; GM Chris Ballard said he will play in the Week 1 opener vs. Baltimore. He is 34 and physically limited to short, underneath routes at this stage.
- **Risk:** He was arrested in downtown Indianapolis around 1 a.m. on Aug 30, 2026 on misdemeanor OVWI and OVWI-endangerment charges, with a court date of Sept 16. The Colts say he'll play Week 1 and won't be internally punished, but the NFL substance-abuse policy allows up to a mandatory three-game suspension on a conviction or guilty plea, and the league typically waits for the legal process. That's the specific thing that breaks this pick. Secondary risk: Downs and Warren already own the underneath targets Allen needs.
- **Draft note:** Late-round WR4/flex in half-PPR — his value is reception volume, which your 0.5 PPR partly rewards. In a 13-round league I'd only take him in the last round or two, and I'd discount him further for the suspension overhang until Sept 16 resolves.
- **Confidence:** medium
