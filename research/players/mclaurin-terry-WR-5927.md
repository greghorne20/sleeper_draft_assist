## Terry McLaurin — WR, WAS, bye 7  (player_id: 5927)

- **Role:** starter (clear WR1 in Washington)
- **Situation:** 2025 was a disaster: 38 catches, 582 yards and 3 TDs on 60 targets in 10 games, WR36 in PPR points per game. The causes were stacked — a contract holdout that cost him most of camp, an ankle issue that put him on PUP until mid-August, a three-year extension signed a week later, then a Week 3 quad injury that recurred and cost him seven of the next eight games. Washington cycled through three quarterbacks (Jayden Daniels missed 10 games with a knee sprain and dislocated elbow) and finished 29th in pass rate, 31st in attempts, 29th in neutral pass rate. For 2026: Daniels is back and healthy, David Blough is the new offensive coordinator, and Washington enters near the top of the league in vacated targets and air yards with Zach Ertz and Deebo Samuel gone. Stefon Diggs was signed and is the main new target competition; Dyami Brown, Van Jefferson and Treylon Burks came in on vet minimums. McLaurin ran a career-high yards per route run even in a bad year.
- **Health:** Healthy. He participated fully in voluntary OTAs with his contract settled, reported to camp on time, and described himself in early August as "hold-in and injury free." No current designation.
- **Risk:** Age-31 receiver whose two prior seasons bracket a 13-TD outlier and a 3-TD collapse — the honest range is wide. The specific failure mode is another Daniels injury: McLaurin and Daniels shared the field for only three games in 2025 and the results in that small sample were poor. Secondary risk is Diggs cutting into the underneath work if he becomes a Daniels favorite.
- **Draft note:** A WR23-ish ADP for a healthy WR1 on a team with this much vacated volume is fair-to-good in 0.5 PPR. He's a solid WR2 with a real bounce-back case; the format's half-point receptions mute both the ceiling and the downside here.
- **Confidence:** high
