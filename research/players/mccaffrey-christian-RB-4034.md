## Christian McCaffrey — RB, SF, bye 8  (player_id: 4034)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Still the centerpiece of Kyle Shanahan's offense after leading the NFL with 413 touches, 2,126 scrimmage yards and 17 TDs in 2025. He missed practice from Aug. 8 into mid-August with "tightness," which sparked hold-in speculation given his non-guaranteed $12.5M salary, but he said it was a planned ramp-up for the Week 1 opener in Melbourne. Signed through 2027.
- **Health:** Back in team drills and says he feels great; no current injury designation. History of Achilles and calf problems from 2024.
- **Risk:** Age 30 plus an extensive soft-tissue history — one calf or Achilles setback erases the value of a first-round pick, and the 413-touch workload cuts both ways.
- **Draft note:** Late Round 1 / early Round 2, roughly picks 8–14; elite receiving usage keeps the ceiling first-round.
- **Confidence:** medium-high
