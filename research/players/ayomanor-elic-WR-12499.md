## Elic Ayomanor — WR, TEN, bye 9  (player_id: 12499)

- **Role:** committee / rotational — one of five WRs on the 53, fighting for the No. 3 or No. 4 job
- **Situation:** He was Tennessee's de facto No. 1 by the end of his rookie year (41 catches on 89 targets, 515 yards, four TDs in 16 games with 14 starts, after Calvin Ridley broke his fibula), and the Titans then spent the entire offseason replacing him. Carnell Tate went No. 4 overall, Wan'Dale Robinson signed as a free agent, and Ridley's contract was restructured to keep him. That pushes Ayomanor to roughly third or fourth in the pecking order, in a straight fight with fellow second-year man Chimere Dike for the No. 3 role. New staff throughout — Robert Saleh as HC, Brian Daboll as OC. Camp reports in July had him "continuing to impress." Note that the offense he's in ranked 30th in passing yards and 31st in passing TDs last season.
- **Health:** No injury designation found. He's on the initial 53 and was practicing through camp.
- **Risk:** The straightforward one: he's the fourth option in a bad passing offense and the volume that made him rosterable last year came entirely from injuries ahead of him. His efficiency was genuinely poor — 5.8 yards per target on a team-leading target count is close to the worst possible combination, and it means even if the targets come back he may not convert them. Year-2 breakout is a real pattern, but he needs two of Tate, Robinson and Ridley to fail or get hurt for it to matter.
- **Draft note:** Undraftable in a 12-team, 13-round league — he was ranked around WR108 by one outlet and WR125 by another, meaning consensus expects him to go undrafted. Leave him on the wire and watch the first three weeks; if Tate stumbles or Ridley's leg flares, he's the first Titans WR to add.
- **Confidence:** medium — roster spot and demotion are well documented, but "No. 3 or No. 4" is a real ambiguity nobody has resolved and I found no reporting on how the new staff plans to deploy him specifically.
