## Kenneth Walker — RB, KC, bye 5  (player_id: 8151)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Walker signed with Kansas City this offseason on a large contract after winning Super Bowl LX MVP with Seattle (417 total yards and four TDs across that postseason run). Isiah Pacheco and Kareem Hunt both left, so there's no established competition — the depth behind him is Brashard Smith, Emari Demercado and fifth-round rookie Emmett Johnson, whom The Athletic called an early camp "faller." Eric Bieniemy is back as OC; his last two years running the Chiefs offense (2021–22) were 11% and 13% more pass-heavy than expected. Walker's 2025 was suppressed in Seattle by usage, not ability: he had a 31.9% route share to Zach Charbonnet's 34.7%, and only 34.8% of RB red-zone carries when Charbonnet was active.
- **Health:** He missed a practice in late August with a foot issue that the Chiefs attributed to his shoe/cleat, and separately dealt with ankle swelling described as minor. No Week 1 designation found. He has a history of injury-interrupted seasons.
- **Risk:** Andy Reid has historically used a different back on passing downs, and Walker has posted a sub-8% target share in three of four seasons — so the half-PPR receiving bump may never arrive. Add that he's never scored more than nine TDs in a season, and the Chiefs are working Patrick Mahomes back from a torn ACL, which caps the offense's early-season ceiling.
- **Draft note:** ADP ~19 overall / RB12 is rich for a back whose case rests entirely on projected volume in a new scheme. Round 2 only if you're comfortable paying for the situation rather than the production; I'd prefer Chase Brown at a similar cost in this format.
- **Confidence:** medium — the role is clear, but the passing-down share and Mahomes' rehab both materially affect his projection and neither is settled.
