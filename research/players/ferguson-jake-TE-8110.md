## Jake Ferguson — TE, DAL, bye 14  (player_id: 8110)

- **Role:** starter (unquestioned Cowboys TE1; Brevyn Spann-Ford and Luke Schoonmaker behind him)
- **Situation:** Ferguson is locked in as Dallas' every-week starting tight end after a career-high 82 catches on 102 targets for 600 yards and 8 TDs in 2025, but he is the third option behind CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens when the offense is whole — he averaged 5.3 targets and 4 catches per game in the 13 games he shared the field with a healthy Lamb, and much of his 2025 value came in the four games Lamb missed or exited. Nothing changed structurally this offseason: Lamb and Pickens both return, Javonte Williams was extended, Brian Schottenheimer enters year two, and camp buzz around WR Ryan Flournoy is a new minor threat to the target scraps.
- **Health:** No known injury. Sat out all three preseason games along with Prescott, Lamb and Pickens as a precaution; expected available for Week 1 vs. the Giants on Sept. 13. He missed one practice in camp for personal reasons.
- **Risk:** He is a volume-dependent short-area target (4.7 aDOT, ~1.26 yards per route run, under 6 yards per target in each of the last two years) whose 2025 value was propped up by outlier red-zone touchdowns — second among TEs in red-zone targets. If the TD rate regresses and Lamb/Pickens stay healthy, he's a 5-target-per-game TE with no yardage ceiling. His second-half 2025 split is the warning: 7.3 PPG over his final eight healthy games.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR with no TE premium, he's a mid-range TE1/high TE2 — fair around the TE10–13 range, roughly rounds 9–11 of a 13-round draft. Given you only start one TE and have six bench spots, he's a reasonable "settle" TE rather than a target.
- **Confidence:** high
