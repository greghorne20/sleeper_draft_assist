## De'Von Achane — RB, MIA, bye 6  (player_id: 9226)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** The clear alpha of Miami's backfield and the centerpiece of the offense after the Tyreek Hill and Jaylen Waddle departures, coming off 1,838 scrimmage yards (1,350 rushing, 67 catches) and signing a four-year extension worth roughly $64–68M. New HC Jeff Hafley and OC Bobby Slowik want a more run-heavy scheme built around him; Malik Willis is now the QB, with Jaylen Wright and Ollie Gordon II behind him. His 145 receptions over the past two seasons lead all NFL backs.
- **Health:** Returned from offseason shoulder surgery, cleared for camp with no limitations, practicing in full-contact team periods and sharp in brief preseason action.
- **Risk:** A downgraded QB room meaning stacked boxes and fewer scoring chances, plus a modest red-zone role (only 12 total TDs on enormous volume) and durability questions on a light frame.
- **Draft note:** Round 1–2, roughly picks 8–16; the receiving volume is a major half-PPR asset.
- **Confidence:** high
