## Cam Ward — QB, TEN, bye 9  (player_id: 12522)

- **Role:** starter (unquestioned; Mitchell Trubisky and Will Levis behind him)
- **Situation:** Ward's rookie year was among the worst in the league — 3,169 yards, 15 TDs, 7 INTs in 17 games, 186.4 passing yards per game (31st of 42), 5.9 yards per attempt (last among qualifiers), and bottom-tier EPA per play, sack rate and success rate. He also took a league-high 55 sacks. The one encouraging signal: over his last four full starts he averaged 15.5 fantasy points per game with two TD passes in each of three straight games. Tennessee rebuilt around him — Robert Saleh is the new head coach, Brian Daboll the new OC, and the team drafted Carnell Tate (No. 4 overall) and signed Wan'Dale Robinson to a four-year, $70M deal to join Calvin Ridley. The offensive line returns three starters (Dan Moore, J.C. Latham, Peter Skoronski) but remains a projected weak spot at the other two positions.
- **Health:** No injury reported.
- **Risk:** He may simply not be good enough yet. The 2025 tape was bad by nearly every advanced metric, and the offensive line is still projected as subpar-to-bad, which is what produced the 55 sacks. Daboll's Giants offenses shifted run-heavy with poor QB play — that's the likely template here if Ward struggles again, which caps his volume.
- **Draft note:** Leave him undrafted in a 12-team 1-QB league — no one is starting him at 4-pt pass TDs and 1 point per 25 yards. Worth watching on waivers for the December stretch (Commanders, Lions, Raiders) if he shows early sparks.
- **Confidence:** high
