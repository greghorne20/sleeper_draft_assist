## Drake Maye — QB, NE, bye 11  (player_id: 11564)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Coming off a breakout second season in which he led the NFL in completion percentage and took New England to Super Bowl LX (lost to Seattle). Mike Vrabel and Josh McDaniels return; the receiving corps was overhauled with A.J. Brown acquired by trade from Philadelphia and Romeo Doubs signed, while Stefon Diggs — the 2025 team receiving leader — was released. Named a captain for a second straight year.
- **Health:** Healthy; a full camp participant who took no preseason snaps. No injury noted.
- **Risk:** New pass-catchers needing time to gel and a third-year leap that isn't guaranteed — at 4-point passing TDs he needs his rushing production to stay meaningful to be a difference-maker.
- **Draft note:** QB1 upside at a discount in this format — rounds 7–10 is the value window.
- **Confidence:** medium-high
