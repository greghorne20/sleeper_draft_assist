## C.J. Stroud — QB, HOU, bye 8  (player_id: 9758)

- **Role:** starter (unquestioned; Davis Mills is the only other QB on the 53)
- **Situation:** Stroud is Houston's Week 1 starter vs. Buffalo on Sept. 13 and GM Nick Caserio confirmed the job was never in question, though the team has set a Week 1 deadline on extension talks. He's thrown for 3,041 yards, 19 TDs and 8 INTs in 14 starts in 2025 and has finished QB26 and QB20 in points per game the last two years after a QB7 rookie season. Houston did rebuild the offensive line and traded for David Montgomery, but the receiving room got worse late: Jayden Higgins tore his ACL in camp and is out for the year, and Tank Dell opens on IR, leaving Nico Collins, Dalton Schultz, Xavier Hutchinson and rookie Jaylin Noel. Camp reports on Stroud were strong.
- **Health:** No current injury reported. He suffered a concussion during the 2025 season (Mills went 3-0 in relief), which is worth noting as a recurrence risk but is not an active issue.
- **Risk:** The offensive environment actively works against him. Houston has one of the league's best defenses and wants to control possessions — the team rarely produces pass-heavy game scripts, Stroud has never thrown 25 TDs in a season, and he adds essentially nothing on the ground. Right tackle Braden Smith was carted off practice with a foot injury and his Week 1 status is unresolved, so the line rebuild isn't confirmed yet either.
- **Draft note:** Not draftable as your QB1 in a 12-team 1-QB league at 4-pt pass TDs and 1 point per 25 yards — his ADP has fallen to around QB22. Stream or take him as a very late QB2/bye-week body only.
- **Confidence:** high
