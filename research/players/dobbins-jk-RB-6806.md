## J.K. Dobbins — RB, DEN, bye 10  (player_id: 6806)

- **Role:** starter — atop Denver's depth chart, in a committee with RJ Harvey
- **Situation:** Dobbins re-signed on a two-year, $16M deal and is the clear No. 1 entering the season. Through the first 10 games of 2025 he ran for 772 yards and 4 TDs at 5.0 yards per carry, getting at least 14 carries in nine of those ten games and out-carrying Harvey 153-50 overall and 19-6 in the red zone before a Lisfranc foot injury in Week 10 ended his year. Denver returned its top four rushers and added fourth-rounder Jonah Coleman, who projects as a third-down candidate behind Dobbins and Harvey. Sean Payton has been public about wanting to run the ball better. Dobbins was a full participant in camp with no injury designation.
- **Health:** Currently healthy — full camp participant, no designation. The history is the concern: he has never played more than 15 games in five NFL seasons, appearing in only 47 career games. He missed all of 2021 with a major knee injury, was limited to eight games in 2022 with further knee issues, tore his Achilles in 2023, and lost 2025 from Week 10 on to the Lisfranc.
- **Risk:** He gets hurt again and Harvey inherits the job — which is exactly what happened last year, when Harvey averaged 15.6 points per game on 16.9 touches and a 60.7% snap rate over the final seven weeks. Even healthy, Dobbins is not a receiving back (70 catches on 652 career touches), so Harvey keeps a pass-game role that limits Dobbins' half-PPR ceiling and gives Denver an easy off-ramp if the veteran slows. There's also a live possibility the Broncos deliberately cap his workload at 27 given the injury history.
- **Draft note:** An RB2 with a genuinely wide range in this scoring — the carries and goal-line work are real, but the missing receptions cost him in 0.5 PPR and the games-played history is the worst in this batch. Fair around rounds 4–6, and pair him with Harvey if you can.
- **Confidence:** high
