## Justin Jefferson — WR, MIN, bye 6  (player_id: 6794)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Jefferson is still the unquestioned alpha in Minnesota — 28.5% target share (6th) and 35.1% first-read share (7th) in 2025 — but he finished WR28 in fantasy points per game on 84-of-140 for 1,048 yards and two TDs, wrecked largely by quarterback play. The big offseason change: Arizona released Kyler Murray, he signed a one-year prove-it deal with Minnesota, and Kevin O'Connell named him the Week 1 starter over J.J. McCarthy. Jordan Addison, Jauan Jennings and T.J. Hockenson round out the pass-catching group.
- **Health:** No reported injury; nothing surfaced in searches suggesting he's limited entering Week 1. (Treat as healthy but unconfirmed by an official report.)
- **Risk:** The quarterback fix doesn't take. Murray is coming off an injury-interrupted 2025 and Fantasy Life ranks him only QB16; if he gets hurt or benched, McCarthy — 34th of 38 in PFN QB Impact last year — is back under center and Jefferson repeats his 2025. There's also a real signal in Jefferson's own metrics: 36th in separation score, 38th in route win rate, tied for 6th-most drops. That isn't purely situational.
- **Draft note:** Late-first/early-second in this format (ADP ~12); the price already assumes the bounce-back, so he's fine but not a bargain at the 1/2 turn.
- **Confidence:** high
