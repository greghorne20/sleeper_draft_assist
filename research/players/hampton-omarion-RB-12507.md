## Omarion Hampton — RB, LAC, bye 7  (player_id: 12507)

- **Role:** starter, but in a stated committee
- **Situation:** Hampton is comfortably atop the Chargers' depth chart entering Year 2 behind a revamped offensive line and new OC Mike McDaniel. The complication is explicit: McDaniel has said publicly he expects Hampton, Keaton Mitchell and Kimani Vidal all to play every game and that the backfield could be dictated by the hot hand. In his four healthy games in 2025 (Weeks 1–4) he averaged 17 touches and 95 total yards as the RB13 in PPG with a 10.1% target share; he also took ~80% of snaps inside the five. McDaniel's offenses have ranked 3rd, 3rd and 7th in RB target share the last three years.
- **Health:** He fractured his left ankle in Week 5 of 2025, missed until Week 14, then a separate right-ankle injury cost him the finale. He was clearly not 100% on return — only one game above a 55% snap rate. No current injury reported for Week 1. (Note: he also broke the same left ankle in high school in 2021.)
- **Risk:** The committee is real, not coach-speak. If Mitchell and Vidal each hold a defined role, Hampton is a 14-16 touch back at an RB7-RB9 draft price. He also did not have a 30-yard gain after returning last season, which is a fair worry about the ankle's explosiveness.
- **Draft note:** ADP ~pick 13 / RB7; that's a full round richer than I'd want in half-PPR. Round 2 is defensible, Round 1 is not.
- **Confidence:** high
