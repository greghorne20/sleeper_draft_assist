## Ashton Jeanty — RB, LV, bye 13  (player_id: 12527)

- **Role:** starter (workload share for Week 1 uncertain due to injury)
- **Situation:** Jeanty has been the lead back throughout camp and enters Year 2 with a materially better setup: new HC/play-caller Klint Kubiak brings a wide-zone scheme, and Las Vegas signed three-time Pro Bowl C Tyler Linderbaum and Spencer Burford after cycling through 11 O-line starters in 2025. As a rookie he handled 321 touches (975 rush yards, 55 catches, 346 receiving yards, 10 total TDs) and led all backs in opportunity share while finishing RB15 in PPG. Fourth-round rookie Mike Washington Jr. has impressed and Kubiak has explicitly declined to commit to snap percentages. Kirk Cousins opens at QB with No. 1 pick Fernando Mendoza behind him.
- **Health:** Sprained ankle suffered in a non-contact drill on Aug. 23 — he had to be helped off the field. Schefter reported it's a sprain, not a season-altering injury, and he had not returned to full practice as of the first week of September. Kubiak said he's optimistic for the Miami opener but the team won't commit. Treat as a genuine game-time question.
- **Risk:** The ankle lingers into September and Washington eats into the workload while Jeanty is less than 100%. Secondary risk: 3.7 YPC as a rookie was ugly, and if the line improvement doesn't show up, the efficiency doesn't either.
- **Draft note:** His price has slipped from the 1/2 turn; in half-PPR I'd take him early-to-mid Round 2 and be ready to handcuff Washington, given 6 bench spots gives room for it.
- **Confidence:** medium — role is clear, but the Week 1 availability is unresolved as of today.
