## Fernando Mendoza — QB, LV, bye 13  (player_id: 13269)

- **Role:** backup (QB2 behind Kirk Cousins)
- **Situation:** The No. 1 overall pick of the 2026 draft out of Indiana (2025 Heisman winner, national champion) is opening the season on the bench. Head coach Klint Kubiak named Kirk Cousins the Week 1 starter on Wednesday of cut-down week; Aidan O'Connell is the third QB. Las Vegas traded Geno Smith to the Jets in the offseason to clear the path for Mendoza, and both the coaching staff and Mendoza himself have publicly accepted a sit-and-learn plan. Beat expectation is that he starts at some point — Silver and Black Pride's mailbag pegs a realistic first start around Week 8 vs. the Jets, and PFT's Florio said it would be a major surprise if Mendoza isn't playing by season's end. Kubiak had at one point declined to even guarantee Mendoza the QB2 job, which is a real signal about where the staff thinks he is.
- **Health:** No reported injury.
- **Risk:** He may not start until midseason or later, and the situation he inherits is bad — the Raiders went 3-14 in 2025 behind an offensive line that allowed 55 sacks, and the line rebuild has produced mixed early returns. His preseason was rough (26-of-46, 250 yards, 1 TD, 2 INT). A stashed QB who doesn't play until Week 8 and then struggles behind a bad line is a wasted roster spot in a 13-round league.
- **Draft note:** Undraftable in a 12-team, 1-QB, 13-round league — leave him in free agency and add him off waivers if/when he's named starter. Your scoring (4-pt pass TDs, 1 point per 25 pass yards, -2 INTs) further blunts any rookie-QB appeal.
- **Confidence:** high
