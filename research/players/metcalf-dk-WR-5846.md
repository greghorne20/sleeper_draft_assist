## DK Metcalf — WR, PIT, bye 9  (player_id: 5846)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Still Pittsburgh's nominal WR1 — Aaron Rodgers said publicly in camp that the team has to get him the ball because "he's our No. 1 for a reason." But 2025 was a career-low fantasy year (59-850-6 on 99 targets, WR26 in PPR, WR19 in PPG) and the offseason added competition rather than help: Pittsburgh traded for Michael Pittman Jr., who has cleared 111 targets in each of the last five seasons. Mike McCarthy replaced Mike Tomlin as head coach, which is what brought Rodgers back for a final year at age 42–43. Metcalf's aDOT fell from 13.5 in his last Seattle season to 10.5 in 2025 — half his yardage came after the catch, not on the deep balls he's built for. He restructured his contract this offseason.
- **Health:** Missed several weeks of camp with an undisclosed injury. GM Omar Khan confirmed he'll be available for Week 1 vs. Atlanta, but ESPN reporting says he'll be "eased in," which raises the possibility of a snap count in the opener. Pittman is also working back from a hamstring.
- **Risk:** Rodgers' arm. He threw for just 3,332 yards in 16 games last year — the lowest full-season output of his career — and a McCarthy offense that leans on the run compresses total attempts further. If Pittman takes over the target lead and Metcalf isn't getting deep shots, his 21.1% target share drops and he becomes a touchdown-dependent WR4 who ruins your lineup decisions weekly. Nine of his 15 games last year finished outside the top 24.
- **Draft note:** WR3 range in half-PPR, so Round 5–6 at the earliest. Worth a look if he slides past ADP; not a player to reach for, and the Week 1 snap-count note argues for tempering expectations in the opener.
- **Confidence:** high
