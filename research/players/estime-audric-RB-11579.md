## Audric Estime — RB, NO, bye 8  (player_id: 11579)

- **Role:** backup — on the active roster, but third or fourth in a crowded backfield
- **Situation:** New Orleans reshaped this room under HC Kellen Moore. They signed Travis Etienne Jr. to a four-year, $48M deal to be the bell cow, kept Alvin Kamara as the second option, and still have Kendre Miller and Ty Chandler (Chandler is on IR). Estime survived to the active roster and appears on current depth charts, but the projected order is Etienne, then Kamara, then reserves. There was a real bright spot: on the first unofficial depth chart he took first-team reps alongside Etienne and Kamara while Miller (back) and Neal (hamstring) were day-to-day, and he closed 2025 strong — 35 carries for 157 yards (4.5 per carry) and a TD across the final two games, good for top-13 fantasy finishes in Weeks 17 and 18. Tyler Shough is the QB.
- **Health:** No injury designation found; he made the 53 and appears on active depth charts. He is not one of the Saints backs listed on IR.
- **Risk:** Traffic, straightforwardly. He's a 227-pound early-down back on a team that just paid $48M for an early-down back and still employs Kamara. His career line across two seasons and three organizations is 508 rushing yards and three TDs, and he's already been cut by Denver and released by Philadelphia's practice squad. His late-2025 usage came entirely from injuries in front of him. One outlet had him at RB106 in the spring.
- **Draft note:** Undraftable in this format — he's a handcuff to a handcuff. The one scenario worth remembering: if Etienne misses time, the Saints' next-man-up order is genuinely unclear between Kamara (34-ish and a receiving back), Miller (durability issues) and Estime, so he's a plausible waiver add rather than a preseason pick. Note his 17 career receptions in two seasons means 0.5 PPR does nothing for him.
- **Confidence:** medium — his roster spot is confirmed across several current depth charts, but the exact ordering behind Etienne and Kamara varies by source and no one has reported a defined role for him.
