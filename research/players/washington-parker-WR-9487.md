## Parker Washington — WR, JAX, bye 7  (player_id: 9487)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Washington broke out after Travis Hunter's Week 7 LCL tear last year, averaging 14.9 PPR points from Week 8 on (16th among WRs over that stretch) and finishing WR27 overall. His YPRR jumped from 1.02 in 2024 to 2.06 in 2025 with a 13.1 aDOT, and he closed with four straight games (including playoffs) of 9+ targets. Jacksonville's first unofficial camp depth chart listed him with the starters, and RotoWire/DraftSharks have him at or near the top of the WR room ahead of Brian Thomas Jr. and Jakobi Meyers. The key structural change: multiple reports have Jacksonville playing Hunter mostly at cornerback in 2026, which is what created Washington's opening in the first place. OC Grant Udinski publicly said Washington is capable of repeating the late-2025 form. He's also playing without a long-term extension — talks with the team remain unresolved less than a week from the opener. He's the listed punt returner too.
- **Health:** No current designation. He returned to practice on Aug. 30 after an unspecified absence and is not listed as a Week 1 concern.
- **Risk:** The breakout sample is small and circumstantial. Three other legitimate target earners (Thomas, Meyers, Brenton Strange) are healthy this year, plus Hunter could get pulled back onto offense at any point. At 5-10 he profiles as a slot, and if Liam Coen's offense redistributes toward Thomas downfield, Washington's target share can fall from ~22% to low teens quickly.
- **Draft note:** Around his WR3-ish ADP he's a fine flex in half-PPR — a Round 5–7 target where the price already reflects the "prove it again" discount, and the fact that he's playing for a contract is a small plus.
- **Confidence:** medium
