## Puka Nacua — WR, LAR, bye 11  (player_id: 9493)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** The Rams' focal point and Matthew Stafford's top target (129 rec, 1,715 yards, 10 TDs in 2025), now playing on a contract year while seeking an extension. Two clouds overlap: a psoas injury from an Aug. 11 joint practice with Dallas that cost him roughly three weeks, and an ongoing NFL Personal Conduct Policy review stemming from a civil lawsuit. The Rams re-acquired Tutu Atwell partly as insurance.
- **Health:** Returned from the psoas injury and got a full week of prep ahead of the Sept. 10 opener vs. San Francisco; appears ready for Week 1.
- **Risk:** A conduct suspension landing mid-season with little warning — he avoided the exempt list on Aug. 31/Sept. 1 but the review is unresolved, and the league can act at any time. Psoas recurrence is the secondary risk.
- **Draft note:** Elite talent at a discount — late Round 1 / early Round 2 rather than top-5 overall, and pair him with a startable bench WR.
- **Confidence:** medium (talent high, availability low)
