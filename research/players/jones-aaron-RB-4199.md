## Aaron Jones — RB, MIN, bye 6  (player_id: 4199)

- **Role:** committee
- **Situation:** (Rostered as Aaron Jones Sr.) Minnesota was preparing to release him in March before he agreed to lower his base salary to $5.5M for one more season. He and Jordan Mason are listed as co-starters on the unofficial depth chart, with Jones expected to handle passing downs and Mason early downs — the same split as last year, when Jones out-carried Mason 11.0 to 6.2 per game across their 12 shared games but was the less efficient back (4.2 YPC to Mason's 4.8). Jones finished RB41 in PPR at 9.9 PPG. The camp reporting has moved against him: several outlets describe Mason as looking like the clear RB1 and "taking a leap." Kevin O'Connell gave Mason goal-line work last season and Mason converted six rushing TDs. The offense should be better with Kyler Murray now at QB in place of J.J. McCarthy.
- **Health:** No current designation found. Durability is the running theme though — five games missed in 2025 (132 carries, his lowest since his rookie year) and six missed in 2024.
- **Risk:** Mason simply takes the job. Jones is 31 with well over 1,500 career carries and hasn't broken a run longer than 41 yards since 2021. If the split tilts and Mason keeps the goal line, Jones is a 8-carry, 3-target back — which in half-PPR (not full) further softens the pass-game floor that's his main argument.
- **Draft note:** Round 11–12 as RB depth in half-PPR. The receiving role gives him a usable bye-week floor; the upside case requires a Mason injury.
- **Confidence:** medium
