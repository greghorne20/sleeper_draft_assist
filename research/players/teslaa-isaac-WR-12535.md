## Isaac TeSlaa — WR, DET, bye 6  (player_id: 12535)

- **Role:** starter in three-WR sets — the WR3 behind Amon-Ra St. Brown and Jameson Williams
- **Situation:** Detroit's first unofficial depth chart lists St. Brown, Williams and TeSlaa as the starters, with St. Brown in the slot and Williams and TeSlaa outside; Cedrick Wilson Jr. is behind him. The opening came from Kalif Raymond leaving for Chicago, and the Lions' other additions (Greg Dortch, fifth-rounder Kendrick Law) were brought in largely for special teams. Dan Campbell has praised him publicly for being steady and unflappable. His rookie usage was tiny but pointed: 16 catches on 27 targets for 239 yards and six TDs, and he tied for 11th in the entire NFL with 11 end-zone targets. Snaps jumped from 14.4 per game over the first ten weeks to 44.0 per game over the final six after the Lions' tight end injuries, and four of his six TDs came in that stretch. He's 6'3" and was a plus contested-catch player.
- **Health:** No injury designation found. He played all 17 games with three starts as a rookie.
- **Risk:** The target math is unforgiving. St. Brown and Williams took 55% of Detroit's receiving yards in 2025, and Jahmyr Gibbs and Sam LaPorta are both expected to clear 600 yards, which leaves roughly a thousand yards on the table for TeSlaa in the best case. He's never earned volume at this level — a 4.9% target share as a rookie — so the pick is a bet on a role expanding, not one that exists. The specific failure mode is that his value stays TD-dependent: strip out a red-zone hot streak and he's a 35-catch, 450-yard WR5 you can never confidently start.
- **Draft note:** A last-round dart in 0.5 PPR, and honestly closer to a watch-list name than a pick given you only have six bench spots. The good news for your format: half-PPR blunts his low-catch profile less than full PPR would, and six TDs on 16 catches means his per-touch scoring is where the value lives. Take him only after you've filled real bench needs.
- **Confidence:** medium — the depth-chart spot is confirmed, but every projection for his output is speculative and analysts are openly split on whether to draft him at all.
