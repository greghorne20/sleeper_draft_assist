## Cyrus Allen — WR, KC, bye 5  (player_id: 13413)
- **Role:** backup (reserve receiver to open the season)
- **Situation:** Fifth-round rookie (No. 176, Cincinnati) who was one of the genuine standouts of Chiefs training camp — ESPN wrote him up by name in early August, Andy Reid gave him one-on-one coaching between reps, and Xavier Worthy called him "an electric route runner." He's behind Rashee Rice, Worthy, Jalen Royals and Tyquan Thornton in a crowded room. Preseason production was modest (6 catches on 12 targets for 39 yards), and reporting is that he opens 2026 locked into a reserve role.
- **Health:** No injury reported. Active on the roster and played all three preseason games.
- **Risk:** Camp hype hasn't translated to snaps yet, and Kansas City's target tree is already three-deep with higher-drafted players. A fifth-rounder who's WR4/WR5 on a team with a settled top three can go a whole season under 20 targets.
- **Draft note:** Not draftable in a 13-round league — he's rostered in about 5% of ESPN leagues for a reason. Watchlist name if Rice or Worthy misses time; the Mahomes attachment is the only thing that makes him interesting.
- **Confidence:** medium
