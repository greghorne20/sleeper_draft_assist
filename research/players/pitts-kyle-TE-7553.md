## Kyle Pitts — TE, ATL, bye 11  (player_id: 7553)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Coming off a career year (118 targets, 88-928-5, TE2 overall / TE6 per-game in half-PPR), Pitts is the clear No. 2 target behind Drake London in an Atlanta receiver room that has no established WR2 (Jahan Dotson, Olamide Zaccheaus, rookie Zachariah Branch competing). Big offseason changes: Kevin Stefanski replaced the old regime as HC with Tommy Rees calling the offense, Atlanta traded for Tua Tagovailoa to compete with Michael Penix Jr. (Nov. ACL), and Pitts converted the franchise tag into a three-year, $53M extension. Stefanski's Cleveland offenses ranked 2nd in league TE targets from 2020–25, and camp reporting has Pitts in a "featured role" with heavy 12 personnel usage planned.
- **Health:** No injury designation. He caught all three targets for 59 yards in the preseason win over Miami and has missed only one game across five NFL seasons.
- **Risk:** QB quality. Much of his 2025 production came with Kirk Cousins throwing; Tagovailoa/Penix is a real downgrade in an offense that scored just 20.8 PPG (24th) last year. Stefanski's Falcons also project run-leaning around Bijan Robinson, which caps total pass volume. If London stays healthy all season and the offense stalls, Pitts is a 5-target-per-game TE1 by name only.
- **Draft note:** Half-PPR with no TE premium keeps him honest — he's a mid-range TE1 you take after the McBride/Bowers/Warren/Loveland tier, roughly the 5th–7th TE off the board, and you don't reach into the top 60 picks for him.
- **Confidence:** high
