## Jakobi Meyers — WR, JAX, bye 7  (player_id: 5947)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Acquired from Las Vegas at the 2025 deadline and immediately productive: 42-483-3 on 61 targets in nine games, WR17 in half-PPR from Weeks 11–18. Even in the crowded stretch run with Brian Thomas Jr. back and Parker Washington rolling, Meyers was WR28 in PPG over Weeks 13–18 on a 22.6% target share and 26.7% first-read share. This is his first full offseason in Liam Coen's offense. He's the short-to-intermediate, over-the-middle option — five straight 800+ yard seasons, but only 23 TDs in 78 games since 2021, and he's never finished better than WR19.
- **Health:** Right hand/thumb. He jammed it in a joint practice with Carolina on Aug. 19, wore a brace, missed both remaining preseason games and roughly two weeks of work, then returned to limited practice on Sept. 2. Coen called him "all good." He's on track for Week 1 vs. Cleveland, but he's had very little full-speed work recently.
- **Risk:** Target competition, plainly. Washington, Thomas, Brenton Strange and whatever offensive role Travis Hunter gets all pull from the same pool, and Meyers is the one with the lowest aDOT and the worst TD equity of the group. He's the safest floor and the lowest ceiling; in half-PPR the reduced reception value dulls his best trait.
- **Draft note:** ADP has sagged to around WR47 on the hand injury, which makes him a solid Round 9–10 flex/bench value in half-PPR — a bye-week plug more than a starter.
- **Confidence:** medium
