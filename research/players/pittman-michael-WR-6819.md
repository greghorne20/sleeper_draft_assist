## Michael Pittman — WR, PIT, bye 9  (player_id: 6819)

- **Role:** starter (Steelers WR2 behind DK Metcalf, with Germie Bernard and Roman Wilson behind them)
- **Situation:** Pittsburgh traded for Pittman in March after Indianapolis extended Alec Pierce. He's coming off 80 catches for 784 yards and a career-high 7 TDs in 17 games — his lowest yardage since his rookie year, but he was the WR9 in points per game through Week 10 before Daniel Jones' injury tanked the Colts' offense (he cleared 40 receiving yards once after that). The projected fit is good on paper: Pittman's 8.2 aDOT pairs with a 42-year-old Aaron Rodgers whose own aDOT was 6.1 last season, and Mike McCarthy replaces Mike Tomlin as head coach, which should mean more plays and a more pass-leaning attack than the Steelers' recent norm (they were 28th in total plays in 2025, 57.5% pass rate).
- **Health:** Expected available for Week 1. He missed practice from Aug. 8 with a hamstring injury and returned to camp practice in pads in late August; GM Omar Khan announced he'll be available. Some outlets flagged a possible early snap count for both him and Metcalf, and one listed him as questionable for Week 1. He played through a back fracture in 2025 in addition to a hamstring issue.
- **Risk:** He and Metcalf both arrive without a proven claim on targets in this offense, and Rodgers at 42 is a checkdown thrower on a team that may still be run-first. Draft Sharks pegs Pittman's realistic ceiling at a 24% target share, which historically produced WR18–22 seasons with only four TD catches. The hamstring plus the 2025 back fracture is a real availability question for a possession receiver whose value is entirely volume.
- **Draft note:** WR3/flex in 0.5 PPR — reasonable around WR35–42, roughly rounds 8–10. Half-PPR blunts some of the appeal of a low-aDOT reception merchant relative to full PPR.
- **Confidence:** medium (role and health both have live uncertainty — reports conflict on whether he's fully clear or on a snap count)
