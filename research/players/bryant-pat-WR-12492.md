## Pat Bryant — WR, DEN, bye 10  (player_id: 12492)
- **Role:** committee (in the rotation as roughly WR3/WR4, ahead of Franklin)
- **Situation:** 2025 third-rounder who worked his way up to Denver's No. 3 job late in his rookie year, averaging six targets per game over his final four regular-season contests before injuries (31-378-1 on 49 targets in 15 games). The Waddle trade caps his ceiling, but camp reporting was positive and the Broncos' first unofficial depth chart slotted him fourth overall and second behind Sutton on his side, ahead of Franklin and Humphrey. Sean Payton offense, Bo Nix at QB, Evan Engram at TE also eating targets.
- **Health:** Rocky end to 2025: a lingering hamstring issue in December, then a severe concussion on a Dec 21 hit that sent him to the hospital by ambulance (neck tests negative), then another concussion in the divisional round. No current injury designation found; he made the 53 and practiced through camp.
- **Risk:** Two documented concussions inside a month last season plus a hamstring history — the durability question is real, and it's the specific thing that would kill the pick. Beyond that, he's the fourth mouth in a room with Sutton, Waddle and Mims plus a receiving TE.
- **Draft note:** Late-round or waiver-wire in half-PPR. With only 13 rounds and one flex slot, he's a bench stash at best; I'd rather have him than Franklin if I'm taking a Denver dart.
- **Confidence:** medium
