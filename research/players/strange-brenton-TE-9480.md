## Brenton Strange — TE, JAX, bye 7  (player_id: 9480)

- **Role:** starter (projected Week 1 starter and clear TE1; rookies Nate Boerkircher (2nd round) and Tanner Koziol (5th round) plus Quintin Morris behind him)
- **Situation:** Jacksonville confirmed Strange as the Week 1 starting tight end after cut-down day, and he signed a three-year extension worth up to $48M in June. He posted a career-best 46-540-3 on 60 targets in 12 games in 2025, with a 16% target share — and over the final seven games after returning from IR he played at a full-season pace of roughly 63-816-7. Liam Coen has said he wants to get Strange "out in space more." The problem is the room around him: Brian Thomas Jr., Jakobi Meyers, Parker Washington and Travis Hunter are all healthy and were never on the field together in 2025, which makes Strange the fourth or fifth option in the pecking order. Jacksonville also plans heavy multi-TE usage.
- **Health:** No current injury reported and he's the projected Week 1 starter. He missed five games in 2025 with a hip injury that landed him on IR.
- **Risk:** Goal-line irrelevance combined with target competition. He's never cleared three TDs or four end-zone targets in a season, and a 16% target share only produced 9.8 PPG (18th among TEs) even when healthy. With four legitimate receivers in front of him, weekly usage becomes a guessing game. The hip injury history is a secondary concern.
- **Draft note:** Low-ceiling TE2 in 0.5 PPR — going around TE17–20, so round 12–13 at most. He's a bye-week fill or a stream, not a set-and-forget starter at your one TE slot.
- **Confidence:** high
