## Jameson Williams — WR, DET, bye 6  (player_id: 8148)

- **Role:** starter (WR2 behind Amon-Ra St. Brown, primary deep threat)
- **Situation:** Career-best 2025: 65 catches, 1,117 yards and 7 TDs on 102 targets across 17 games, a WR12 finish and back-to-back 1,000-yard seasons. His usage is narrow by design — the Lions use him to stretch the field while St. Brown (172 targets, 1,401 yards) takes the safer looks, and he's operated under a 19% target share in each of the last two years. The season split badly: 17 catches for 289 yards through the Week 8 bye, then a surge that coincided with Sam LaPorta's season-ending back injury in Week 10. LaPorta is healthy again. Drew Petzing is the new offensive coordinator and has challenged Williams to play more physically; Goff has praised his added route nuance. Isaac TeSlaa is locked in as WR3. Detroit opens vs. New Orleans on Sept. 13.
- **Health:** Fine. He missed two padded practices in mid-August with a minor shoulder injury — Dan Campbell said "he's got a little shoulder, he's good to go" — and returned to practice Aug. 19 with light limitation. Nothing since.
- **Risk:** Volatility, and the LaPorta variable specifically. He had seven games under 7.0 PPR points last year including two zeros, and has scored 15+ PPR points in only 28% of his career games. His production leans on 17.2-17.3 yards per reception, which is borderline unsustainable, and he led the NFL with 12 drops. When LaPorta, St. Brown and Jahmyr Gibbs have all been available, Detroit has historically used Williams in a low-volume downfield role — that's the exact configuration for 2026.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR, a low-catch big-play profile is less penalized than in full PPR, and 6-point TDs on 30-yard average scoring plays help. He's a WR3/flex with WR2 weeks — draft him as a swing piece, not as someone you need to start every week.
- **Confidence:** medium
