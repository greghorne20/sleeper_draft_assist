## Chuba Hubbard — RB, CAR, bye 5  (player_id: 7594)

- **Role:** starter, but opening the year in a committee with Jonathon Brooks
- **Situation:** Hubbard is the listed starter and Rico Dowdle's departure to Pittsburgh reopened the backfield he lost control of last year. In 2024 he ran for 1,195 yards and 10 TDs on 250 carries in 15 starts; in 2025 a calf injury cost him the job — he started only the first six of 15 games and fell from 13.2 carries and 47 yards per game to 6.1 and 25.4 over his final nine. He's on a four-year extension through 2028 and is one of the team's veteran leaders. Carolina won the NFC South at 8-9 last season. The Athletic reports Dave Canales will open with a committee, with Brooks second and Trevor Etienne, AJ Dillon and Anthony Tyrus III behind.
- **Health:** Strained hamstring suffered in one-on-ones on Aug. 12; Canales called it "a legit hamstring" and week-to-week. Hubbard has not played a preseason snap. He returned to practice on Sept. 1 and the team expects him ready for the Sept. 13 opener against Chicago — he's hit over 20 mph in controlled settings — but Carolina has held him out of contact work and admits he hasn't been cutting freely. Hubbard himself noted he came back too early from last year's calf and that it lingered.
- **Risk:** Two compounding things. First, a hamstring that hasn't been contact-tested with a player who has a recent history of rushing back from soft-tissue injury and losing the job as a result. Second, the ceiling: he ranked 64th of 65 qualified backs in both forced missed tackle rate and elusive rating per PFF in 2025, and Jonathon Brooks — a 2024 second-rounder finally healthy after two ACL tears — is a live threat to take the job outright if Hubbard stumbles early.
- **Draft note:** A flex/bench body in 0.5 PPR, not a starting RB2 — the committee language plus the hamstring means his Week 1–4 volume is a guess. Worth a shot in rounds 8–10 as a bounce-back bet, with the early bye (Week 5) making the timing awkward if he opens slowly.
- **Confidence:** medium
