## Dalton Schultz — TE, HOU, bye 8  (player_id: 5001)

- **Role:** starter (Texans TE1, ~69% snap share; Foster Moreau, Cade Stover and rookie second-rounder Marlin Klein behind him)
- **Situation:** Schultz is coming off career highs of 106 targets and 82 catches for 777 yards and 3 TDs in 17 games (TE10–TE13 by PPR scoring depending on the cut), and Houston rewarded him with a one-year, $12.6M extension through 2027. His situation improved on the target side after camp: Jayden Higgins tore his ACL and is out for the season, and Tank Dell opens the year on IR, which leaves Schultz and Nico Collins as C.J. Stroud's clear go-to options. Working against him: Houston added Moreau in free agency and a second-round rookie TE, and the offense is deliberately conservative behind an elite defense.
- **Health:** Cleared. He exited the January divisional-round loss to New England with a calf injury and was listed as questionable to open camp with that calf, but GM Nick Caserio said in January he'd be fine and Schultz sat out all three preseason games alongside the other offensive starters. No current designation found.
- **Risk:** He is a pure volume tight end with a 7.0 yards-per-target career average and no TD equity — he hasn't scored more than five in a season since 2021. He's entering his age-30 season, the offense wants to shorten games, and if the target share slips even modestly with a crowded TE room, there's no efficiency to fall back on. The other risk is simply that the Texans' offense is bad again.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR with no TE premium, this is a floor play — worth taking around TE12–15, roughly round 11 or later, with the injuries to Higgins and Dell arguing for the upper end of that range early in the season.
- **Confidence:** high
