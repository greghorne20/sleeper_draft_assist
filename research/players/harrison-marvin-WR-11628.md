## Marvin Harrison — WR, ARI, bye 14  (player_id: 11628)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** (Rostered as Marvin Harrison Jr.) Two disappointing seasons in, and 2025 was wrecked by injuries: concussion in Week 6, an appendectomy after Week 9 that cost him ten pounds and two weeks, then a heel/foot problem — five games missed, 41-608-4 on 73 targets. He was still WR27 in PPG through Week 13 and averaged 12.8 PPG across his last five healthy games. Everything around him changed: Kyler Murray was released, Jacoby Brissett is the Week 1 starter, Mike LaFleur is the new HC and playcaller (Rams OC the past three years, previously seven seasons under Kyle Shanahan), rookie Carson Beck is a third-round contingency, and Arizona spent the No. 3 overall pick on RB Jeremiyah Love. LaFleur has said there won't be a defined WR1 and talks about moving guys around the formation. Trey McBride remains the target hog, and Michael Wilson just got paid.
- **Health:** Full health. Camp bumps only — he was in discomfort after a sideline catch in late July, rolled his left ankle in August and practiced two days later, and left a Sunday practice with cramps before returning Tuesday. He caught a 7-yard TD on his only preseason target.
- **Risk:** He's now arguably the third option on his own team. McBride absorbs premium usage regardless of QB, Wilson outproduced him head-to-head last year and just signed a three-year, $75M extension, and Brissett is a 33-year-old journeyman with a 6.6 career YPA on a rebuilding roster that also spent the No. 3 pick on a running back. If LaFleur's offense is efficient rather than voluminous, MHJ's ceiling is a WR3 who scores four or five times.
- **Draft note:** ADP has settled around Round 6–7 (WR31–35), which is roughly right. Take him as your WR3/flex in that window; the appeal is the post-hype price, not a projection you should chase upward.
- **Confidence:** high
