## DeVonta Smith — WR, PHI, bye 10  (player_id: 7525)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Philadelphia's outright WR1 for the first time in his career after A.J. Brown was traded to New England. Brown carried a 29.5–30% target share and roughly 39% of the team's air yards in 2025 — that volume is now unallocated. Smith's own 2025 line was 77-1,008-4 on 113 targets (a 24.3–25.3% share), which still led the team in yards. The replacement pieces are Dontayvion Wicks, Elijah Moore, Marquise Brown and first-round rookie Makai Lemon, who missed time this summer with a hamstring; none profile as an alpha target earner. Dallas Goedert also returns and should absorb some of the vacated looks. New OC Sean Mannion runs a Shanahan/McVay-style system. The historical evidence is strong: in games without A.J. Brown over the last two years Smith earned 29%+ target shares, averaged 16.2–16.6 PPG and posted a 2.6 yards-per-route-run mark.
- **Health:** No reported injury. He's been durable, missing only four games across five NFL seasons.
- **Risk:** Team pass volume. Philadelphia attempted only 497 passes in 2025 and ranked 27th in attempts; the offense is still built around Hurts' legs and Saquon Barkley. A 30% share of a bottom-five passing volume is a WR2, not a WR1 — that's the specific way this pick disappoints.
- **Draft note:** Third-round consensus (WR11–16 depending on ranker) for a player with a nearly guaranteed target-share spike and an ironman record; in half-PPR he's one of the safer picks in that range.
- **Confidence:** high
