## Troy Franklin — WR, DEN, bye 10  (player_id: 11627)
- **Role:** backup (WR5/WR6 on the depth chart, possible gameday inactive)
- **Situation:** Franklin was Denver's No. 2 receiver in 2025 by volume (65-709-6, second on the team in targets), but the Broncos traded a first- and a third-round pick for Jaylen Waddle this offseason and that shoved the whole room down. Denver's first unofficial 2026 depth chart (Aug 12) listed him sixth: Sutton, Waddle, Mims, Bryant, Lil'Jordan Humphrey, then Franklin. He made the initial 53 as one of six receivers kept, but local coverage explicitly raised him as a candidate to be a gameday inactive, since he isn't a special-teams contributor. Sean Payton is still the head coach and Bo Nix — Franklin's Oregon teammate — is still the QB.
- **Health:** Left the January 2026 divisional-round game with a hamstring injury and was listed questionable for the AFC Championship. No current 2026 injury designation found; he practiced and made the roster.
- **Risk:** He simply may not play. The role went from ~second in targets to fifth or sixth in the pecking order in one offseason, and camp reporting was that Bryant and Mims outplayed him. A single Sutton or Waddle injury is the only realistic path back to volume.
- **Draft note:** Undraftable in a 13-round, 12-team half-PPR league. Watchlist name only, and only as a Waddle/Sutton contingency.
- **Confidence:** medium
