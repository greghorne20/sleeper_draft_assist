## Woody Marks — RB, HOU, bye 8  (player_id: 12474)

- **Role:** committee — the clear No. 2 behind David Montgomery, with third-down pass-catching duties and a new kickoff-return role
- **Situation:** Marks led the Texans in rushing as a rookie (703 yards, 2 rushing TDs, plus 24 catches for 208 yards and 3 receiving TDs), but Houston traded a fourth-round pick, a 2027 seventh and OL Juice Scruggs to Detroit for Montgomery and then gave him an upgraded deal — a clear statement about who leads this backfield. Beat reporting (Aaron Wilson, KPRC 2) says Marks is adapting to a complementary role that still includes third-down work and carries. The optimistic read is that this is actually a better fit: Marks was one of the most prolific receiving backs in college history (245+ catches at Mississippi State and USC) and was miscast between the tackles last year. He ran the majority of backfield routes in joint practices with the Raiders, and DeMeco Ryans has publicly praised his camp.
- **Health:** No current injury reported. He was nicked up repeatedly as a rookie but missed only one game (ankle).
- **Risk:** Volume. He averaged 3.6 yards per carry as a rookie and finished 48th among RBs in fantasy points per snap. More structurally, Houston's RB target share was 13.2% (29th in the league) on a 57.4% pass rate — even the receiving role he's built for doesn't produce many targets in this offense. Montgomery, meanwhile, has caught passes efficiently and will take goal-line work.
- **Draft note:** A late-round RB4/handcuff in 0.5 PPR — around RB45–50, so rounds 11–13. He's worth more as Montgomery insurance (Montgomery is 29 and missed three games in both 2023 and 2024) than as a standalone flex.
- **Confidence:** high
