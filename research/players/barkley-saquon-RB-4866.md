## Saquon Barkley — RB, PHI, bye 10  (player_id: 4866)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Philadelphia's unquestioned lead back behind an elite offensive line, following a historic 2024 (2,005 rush yards, Super Bowl LIX, OPOY) with a quieter 2025 (1,140 rush yards, 7 TDs). New OC Sean Mannion is installing an outside/wide-zone scheme under a retained Nick Sirianni, and the staff has emphasized getting Barkley more involved as a receiver — potentially his first 300-yard receiving season in Philadelphia. Tank Bigsby looked explosive as the backup.
- **Health:** Healthy; ramped up carefully through camp and held out of preseason contact, with no injury designation.
- **Risk:** The 482 total touches in 2024 raise a workload-regression flag, and a scheme change that takes time to click would blunt his efficiency early.
- **Draft note:** Top-5 overall; an expanded receiving role would raise his half-PPR floor further — worth 1.03–1.06.
- **Confidence:** high
