## Tre Tucker — WR, LV, bye 13  (player_id: 10213)

- **Role:** starter — the closest thing the Raiders have to a lead wideout
- **Situation:** Tucker led Las Vegas with 696 receiving yards in 2025 (57 catches on 92 targets, five TDs) while playing 90–100% of snaps, and he now has the longest tenure of any WR on the roster. He's on the initial 53 alongside Jalen Nailor, Jack Bech, Malik Benson, Dareke Young and Dont'e Thornton Jr. — and Thornton opened the year on IR, off the field for at least four games. Everything around him changed: Klint Kubiak is the new head coach, John Spytek the GM, Kirk Cousins opens as the starting QB with rookie Fernando Mendoza waiting. Kubiak praised him publicly in March. He played 9 of 10 first-team snaps in the preseason opener and caught two passes on the opening drive. The counterweight: Brock Bowers is the actual engine of this passing game, and at least one projection model has Bowers at a 25% target share with Tucker at 15%, tightly bunched with Nailor (14%) and Bech (13%).
- **Health:** No current designation. He hasn't missed a game to injury in three seasons. He did leave a training-camp practice in late summer with an undisclosed leg issue, but he made the 53 and no follow-up limitation was reported; a Week 13 knee issue in 2025 was removed from the report before that weekend.
- **Risk:** The 2025 line flatters him badly. He posted 40.9 fantasy points in a single Week 3 game — roughly a quarter of his season total — and got 60% of his TDs in that one afternoon. Strip that out and he's a 9.5-points-per-game field-stretcher on a team that finished with 5.5 yards per attempt from its QBs. He's 5'9", 182, and his goal-line scoring (3 TDs on 5 targets inside the 10) looks like variance rather than a role. If Cousins is what he was last year, or the target share stays in a three-way tie, Tucker is a WR5 who wins you one week and loses you six.
- **Draft note:** ADP has floated between roughly pick 136 and 150, so he's effectively free in a 13-round, 12-team format. Worth the last pick or two if you want a volume-plus-speed lottery ticket; 0.5 PPR mutes his short-catch value somewhat and his profile is TD-and-explosive dependent, which is the least stable kind of production.
- **Confidence:** medium — role and roster spot are well confirmed; the production case is contested and the QB situation is unresolved.
