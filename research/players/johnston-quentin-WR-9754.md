## Quentin Johnston — WR, LAC, bye 7  (player_id: 9754)

- **Role:** starter (perimeter WR2 behind Ladd McConkey, with Tre' Harris also in the mix)
- **Situation:** Johnston is entering his fourth season on a picked-up fifth-year option after back-to-back 8-TD seasons — 51-735-8 on 84 targets in 14 games in 2025. He opened last year as the fantasy WR4 through a month before a Week 5 hamstring injury gutted his usage (a 9.1% target share and zero targets in Weeks 7–8), and he finished WR27. The big change is the coaching staff: Mike McDaniel is now calling the offense, replacing Greg Roman. Los Angeles also overhauled the tight end room (Oronde Gadsden II plus additions) and the offensive line should be far healthier after losing Rashawn Slater and Joe Alt to season-enders in 2025, when Justin Herbert was pressured on a league-high 43.6% of dropbacks.
- **Health:** Healthy. He had a minor lower-body issue in camp that was not expected to cost practice time, and he played in the preseason finale, catching a 65-yard touchdown from Herbert.
- **Risk:** He's a boom-or-bust TD-dependent receiver on a team where he's the second or third read. McDaniel's offense is expected to lean run-first, McConkey is the clear alpha, and Harris plus a deeper TE room means Johnston never inherits a big target total. If the touchdowns regress from 8, there's no volume floor underneath.
- **Draft note:** A round 10–11 flier in 0.5 PPR (ADP is roughly 96–107, WR38–44) — take him as a bench WR with flex upside, not as a starter you plan around.
- **Confidence:** high
