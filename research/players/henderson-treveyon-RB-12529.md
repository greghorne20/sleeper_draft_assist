## TreVeyon Henderson — RB, NE, bye 11  (player_id: 12529)
- **Role:** committee — the No. 2 back behind Rhamondre Stevenson on the preseason depth chart, with an expected near-even split
- **Situation:** Henderson was excellent as a rookie: 180 carries for 911 yards (5.1 YPC) and nine TDs, plus 35 catches for 221 yards and a score across 17 games (four starts), on a Patriots team that earned the AFC's second seed and reached the Super Bowl. He was PFWA All-Rookie and also returned kicks. But he was barely used in the playoff run, and Stevenson retains Mike Vrabel's trust — Stevenson is listed RB1 on the preseason depth chart. Chad Graff of The Athletic reports Henderson still hasn't separated himself on passing downs and makes just enough pass-protection mistakes that third downs feel risky. JaMycal Hasty, Lan Larison and Hassan Haskins are the depth; the team traded for Corey Kiner at cutdowns. The offense added A.J. Brown, which should draw two-high looks and open running lanes. New England opens the NFL season at Seattle — a Super Bowl rematch — on Wednesday night, Sept. 9.
- **Health:** Not healthy right now. Henderson injured his right ankle slipping on a cut in practice Aug. 24 and had not practiced through Sept. 4. The Boston Globe reported a league source said he avoided serious injury and should be "good to go" for Week 1, and beat reporters describe it as a low ankle issue rather than a high ankle sprain. He has been in the locker room walking normally without a limp. But he did not travel for the preseason finale, missed the first three regular-season practice sessions, and the Patriots had three practices left before departing for Seattle. Status for the opener is genuinely uncertain.
- **Risk:** He misses or is limited in the opener, Stevenson takes the bell-cow role, and Henderson never claws back to a 50-50 split — which is roughly what happened in the 2025 postseason. Even in the good case, the pass-protection issue caps him out of third-down work, which is where the receiving volume lives.
- **Draft note:** Ranked around RB27 before the injury, and the ankle has pushed Stevenson ahead of him on most boards. Half-PPR blunts the loss of receiving work somewhat. He's a round 6-7 RB3/flex with real upside if the ankle clears and the split holds — but with a 6-man bench in a 13-round league, a Week 1 inactive is expensive. Let someone else pay the round 5 price.
- **Confidence:** high on role and situation, low on Week 1 availability.
