## Kyren Williams — RB, LAR, bye 11  (player_id: 8150)

- **Role:** starter in a defined 60/40 rotation
- **Situation:** Williams enters his age-26 season atop the Rams depth chart for a fourth straight year, in an offense that ranked first in the NFL in scoring last season with the reigning MVP (Matthew Stafford, 46 passing TDs) under center. Blake Corum is the clear complement — the split was roughly 60/40 in the back half of 2025, and ESPN's Dan Graziano expects it to narrow toward 50/50 in 2026. Williams still owned the valuable work: 58.6% of red-zone carries, 56.6% inside the 10, an 8.6% target share vs. Corum's 2.4%, and a 47.1% route rate vs. 15.3%. He had 21 of the 27 RB carries and all five RB targets in the playoff win at Chicago.
- **Health:** He and Corum were both listed questionable with ankle injuries late last season and both played. No current injury reported entering Week 1.
- **Risk:** Touchdown dependence plus a shrinking share. He has no explosive plays to fall back on — not one of his 645 touches over the past two regular seasons gained 35+ yards — so if Corum takes goal-line work and Stafford's 46-TD season regresses, Williams' weekly range collapses toward the RB25 area on 13-15 low-yardage touches.
- **Draft note:** Round 3 in half-PPR. His receiving work is real but inefficient (0.81 YPRR, 35th of 42 qualifying backs), so the 0.5-PPR setting doesn't add much to his floor — you're buying volume and TDs, not receptions.
- **Confidence:** high
