## George Pickens — WR, DAL, bye 14  (player_id: 8137)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Starting outside receiver opposite CeeDee Lamb, listed as such on the Cowboys' post-cuts depth chart with Jonathan Mingo behind him. Coming off 93-1,429-9 on 137 targets in 17 games in his first Dallas season, second in the NFL in end-zone targets (18) and fourth in air yards (1,576). What changed: he played out the franchise-tag deadline without an extension, signing a one-year, $27.3M tag in April, so he's in a contract year again. Same QB (Dak Prescott), same head coach (Brian Schottenheimer, year two), new defensive coordinator. His splits with a healthy Lamb were strong on their own — 23% target share and 16.1 fantasy PPG across 12 full games together.
- **Health:** No current injury. He did not play in any of the three preseason games, which appears to be veteran rest rather than an ailment; Fantasy Football Calculator lists no recent injuries. Prior history: a hamstring issue cost him three December games in 2024.
- **Risk:** A fully healthy Lamb plus a Schottenheimer offense that historically leans balanced-to-run-first squeezes his target share back toward the low 20s, and his production has been TD-and-explosive-play dependent rather than volume-driven. The contract-year situation also carries a small in-season distraction risk given his history.
- **Draft note:** Half-PPR flatters his profile relative to full PPR since the value sits in yards and touchdowns; consensus outside rankings have him in the WR10–16 band, so the 2/3 turn is a fair place to take him in this format.
- **Confidence:** high
