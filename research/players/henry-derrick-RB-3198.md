## Derrick Henry — RB, BAL, bye 13  (player_id: 3198)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Entering his age-32 season and 11th year as Baltimore's unquestioned bell cow, averaging 19.6 touches and roughly a TD per game across his two Ravens seasons. New HC Jesse Minter and OC Declan Doyle (from a Bears offense that ranked third in rushing) are reportedly planning more designed perimeter runs and a bit more passing-game work. Justice Hill handles third downs, Rasheen Ali is the depth, and rookie Adam Randall is on IR until at least Week 5.
- **Health:** Healthy; participating fully in camp with no injury designation.
- **Risk:** The age cliff — running backs at 32 tend to decline suddenly rather than gradually, and there's no receiving volume to cushion a drop in rushing efficiency.
- **Draft note:** RB1 in the Round 1–2 range, roughly picks 6–14; the lack of receptions dings him slightly at 0.5 PPR.
- **Confidence:** high
