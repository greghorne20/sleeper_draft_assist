## A.J. Brown — WR, NE, bye 11  (player_id: 5859)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Traded from Philadelphia to New England on June 1 for a 2028 first-round pick and a 2027 fifth. He reunites with HC Mike Vrabel (his Tennessee coach from 2019–21) and steps in as the clear No. 1 target for Drake Maye, who led the NFL in both completion rate (72%) and yards per attempt (8.9) last season. Brown has finished no lower than 7th among WRs in target share in each of the last seven seasons; in 2025 he was 9th at 27.5% with a 35% first-read share. Romeo Doubs is the No. 2 on a big contract but has never topped 724 receiving yards; Hunter Henry hasn't been a top-12 TE since 2020. Projections have Brown near a 27% target share vs. Doubs' 18.3%. Volume should also rise: the Eagles led the NFL in rush attempts per game since 2022 (31.6) while New England ranked 20th (26.1).
- **Health:** A knee issue was reported around the trade, but he is not expected to be limited by it this season, and he has said he's missed essentially one game in four years from a knee shot. No current designation found.
- **Risk:** Team change plus efficiency signals. He was 30th in separation score last year, and Maye spread the ball around in 2025 — the bull case assumes that was purely an absence-of-talent artifact. If New England stays run-leaning against a harder schedule, Brown is a 115-target WR2 at a WR1 price.
- **Draft note:** Round 2 in half-PPR. He's a target-share bet with a legitimately better QB than he had; I'd rather have him than the WRs going right around him.
- **Confidence:** high
