## Matthew Stafford — QB, LAR, bye 11  (player_id: 421)

- **Role:** starter (entrenched)
- **Situation:** Stafford is the reigning NFL MVP. He led the league in passing yards (4,707) and passing touchdowns (46) with 8 INTs and just 23 sacks, finishing QB2–QB4 in fantasy depending on the metric — while rushing for one yard all season. He signed a one-year, $55M extension and is under contract through 2027 with $50M guaranteed. Turning 39 in February, this is his 18th season. The Rams had the No. 1 scoring offense in 2025 and return Puka Nacua and Davante Adams. What changed: Nathan Scheelhaase replaced Mike LaFleur as OC, Kliff Kingsbury joined as assistant head coach, and the Rams drafted QB Ty Simpson 13th overall as the succession plan.
- **Health:** He reports feeling good and had a modified but active training camp — a marked contrast to 2025, when he was sidelined for camp with back trouble and still played all 17 regular-season games plus three playoff games. He has a degenerative back condition; the Athletic's framing is that he ages well as long as the Rams keep hits off that back. The Rams have managed his reps deliberately all offseason.
- **Risk:** Touchdown regression plus the back. Los Angeles generated a league-leading 76 red zone drives in 2025, which is not repeatable, and Stafford threw 16 TDs from inside the five — the only player in the league in double digits there, against just four such scores in 2024. Strip that outlier efficiency and a QB with literally zero rushing production has a thin weekly floor. The age-38 back is the tail risk that ends your season at the position.
- **Draft note:** In a 1-QB, 4-point-TD league, do not pay the MVP tax. His per-attempt production is elite but the format compresses passing value, and the regression case is strong. If he slides to the QB8–12 range (roughly rounds 8–10 here) he's a fine QB1; anything earlier is paying for 2025.
- **Confidence:** high
