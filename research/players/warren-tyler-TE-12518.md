## Tyler Warren — TE, IND, bye 13  (player_id: 12518)

- **Role:** starter (clear TE1, expected to line up from the first snap over Mo Alie-Cox, Drew Ogletree and Will Mallory)
- **Situation:** Massive rookie year — 76 catches on 112 targets for 817 yards and 4 TDs across all 17 games, a Pro Bowl nod, team lead in targets and receiving yards, TE5 overall and TE10 in PPG. He posted a 21.2% target share and ranked top-three among TEs in targets per route run and first-read target rate. The Colts traded Michael Pittman Jr. (110 targets) to Pittsburgh, which vacates a large chunk of volume. Alec Pierce signed an extension but had offseason ankle surgery and has yet to practice, prompting the signing of Keenan Allen as insurance. Daniel Jones is back from the torn Achilles that ended his 2025 and derailed Warren's second half. Warren was the TE4 in expected fantasy points per game last year with only four receiving TDs on the fifth-most red zone targets among TEs.
- **Health:** Cleared. He suffered a mild groin/adductor strain on Aug. 19 in a joint practice with Atlanta, was expected to miss about a week, and returned to practice Tuesday, Sept. 1. Reporting indicates he'll suit up without restrictions for the Week 1 opener vs. Baltimore on Sept. 13.
- **Risk:** The offense is balanced-to-run-leaning — Jonathan Taylor led the NFL in carries in 2025 and the Colts sat mid-pack in pass rate, which isn't expected to expand. The second half of last season is the warning: Warren hit double digits just twice after a torch-hot first seven games, and part of that decline started before Jones went down. If Jones misses time again, or if Allen and a healthy Pierce absorb the vacated Pittman targets rather than Warren, the top-three ceiling evaporates.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR with one TE slot and no TE premium, paying a Round 5 pick for a tight end is steep in a 12-team league — but Warren is the rare TE with a plausible path to a target-share lead on his own team. Fair at TE4/TE5 cost; the positive TD regression case is the reason to take him over the streaming tier.
- **Confidence:** high
