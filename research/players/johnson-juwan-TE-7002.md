## Juwan Johnson — TE, NO, bye 8  (player_id: 7002)

- **Role:** starter (Saints TE1; Noah Fant added in free agency and third-round rookie Oscar Delp behind him, but beat reporting has Johnson firmly atop the depth chart)
- **Situation:** Johnson had a career year in 2025 — 77 catches, 889 yards, 3 TDs on 102 targets in 17 games, second on the team in targets behind Chris Olave, and a TE10–TE12 finish in his first year in Kellen Moore's offense. Year two comes with more target competition on paper: the Saints drafted WR Jordyn Tyson No. 8 overall and have Devaughn Vele. But the early-season picture is favorable — Tyson is on injured reserve with a hamstring and will miss at least the first four games, and Alvin Kamara is out for at least Week 1 with a Grade 2 MCL sprain. Tyler Shough enters his first full season as starter after a solid nine-start close to 2025 (7.6 YPA, 10 TD, 5 INT, 5-4).
- **Health:** No injury reported; he was healthy through camp with beat writers noting big plays. Age-30 season.
- **Risk:** 2025 looks like a perfect storm — Moore's first year, Rashid Shaheed traded, and no other credible pass catcher behind Olave. Once Tyson returns in Week 5 and Kamara is back, Johnson likely settles as the No. 3 option at best. He's also never had more than three touchdowns in a season, so his production is all catches and yards with no scoring equity.
- **Draft note:** A late-round streamer with a real early-season window in 0.5 PPR — he's going around TE16–21, so round 12–13 or waiver-wire territory. The first four weeks are the reason to take him.
- **Confidence:** high
