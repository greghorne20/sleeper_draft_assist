## Jordan Love — QB, GB, bye 11  (player_id: 6804)

- **Role:** starter (entrenched)
- **Situation:** Love threw for 3,381 yards, 23 TDs and just 6 INTs in 2025 (291-of-439, 66.3%) — good for QB15 in total points and only 16.1 per game, QB19 among QBs with eight-plus starts. The disconnect is that the underlying play was excellent: second in EPA per dropback, third in DVOA, third in QBR, sixth in passer rating, and a career-best PFN impact score. The problem is volume. Green Bay finished 31st in pass rate over expected in 2024 and 22nd in 2025. What changed: Doubs and Wicks are gone, concentrating targets on Christian Watson, Matthew Golden, Jayden Reed and a returning Tucker Kraft; Josh Jacobs opens on the commissioner's exempt list; and Micah Parsons is out on defense — all of which point to more trailing, pass-heavy scripts than LaFleur would normally choose.
- **Health:** No current injury reported. He had a concussion late in 2025 that kept him out of Week 18 despite being cleared.
- **Risk:** LaFleur simply doesn't throw enough, and Love contributes almost nothing on the ground (282 rushing yards and one rushing TD across the last two seasons combined). If Jacobs returns and the Packers go back to run-first, Love is a QB18 with no rushing floor to catch him. His pass catchers also all carry injury or consistency questions.
- **Draft note:** ADP around 148 overall / QB18 makes him a late-round QB or a second QB behind someone safer. In this 4-point-TD, 0.04-per-yard format the lack of rushing is a real drag, but at that price he's a fine one-QB-league streaming anchor with genuine top-12 upside if the Jacobs absence forces volume. Last two rounds.
- **Confidence:** high
