## Jacoby Brissett — QB, ARI, bye 14  (player_id: 3257)

- **Role:** starter — listed with the first team and publicly confirmed as QB1 by HC Mike LaFleur
- **Situation:** Arizona released Kyler Murray in March and handed the job to Brissett. They signed Gardner Minshew and drafted Carson Beck in the third round, but Brissett held in through the offseason program and the start of camp and got a revised one-year deal on July 26 worth $15.5M guaranteed (up to $21M with incentives) — money that effectively insures him against being benched early. He's listed atop the first official depth chart. The supporting cast is legitimate: Trey McBride, Marvin Harrison Jr., Michael Wilson, Kendrick Bourne in the slot, and a Jeremiyah Love / Tyler Allgeier backfield. He was one of 2025's genuine fantasy surprises — nine straight top-12 weekly finishes from Weeks 6–15 and 18.9 fantasy points per start over 12 starts, against a prior career high of 12.9 all the way back in 2017.
- **Health:** No injury designation found. He played in the preseason opener against the Raiders, going 5-for-5 for 44 yards with a TD to Harrison.
- **Risk:** Last year's number came with the league's pass-heaviest offense behind it, and that context is gone — Mike LaFleur is now calling the plays and the volume is unlikely to repeat. Brissett also contributes essentially nothing with his legs (career high 260 rushing yards), which in a 4-point-passing-TD league is a real cap on his ceiling. And the leash matters: Beck is a third-round rookie the fan base will start calling for the moment Arizona falls behind in a division that will beat them up. This is a QB2 whose 2025 was a volume artifact.
- **Draft note:** Your league starts one QB with 12 teams, so streaming is viable and there's no reason to pay up. Brissett is a last-round backup or a Week 1 waiver add — and 4-point passing TDs plus 25 yards per point (0.04/yd) further flattens the gap between him and whoever else is on the wire.
- **Confidence:** high — starter status, contract and depth chart are all confirmed by multiple current sources.
