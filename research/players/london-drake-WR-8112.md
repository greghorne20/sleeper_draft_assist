## Drake London — WR, ATL, bye 11  (player_id: 8112)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** London signed a four-year, $141M extension ($100M guaranteed) making him the league's third-highest-paid receiver, and he's the unambiguous focal point of Atlanta's passing game. Through Weeks 1–10 of 2025 he was the WR6 in PPG with a 30.3% target share before a PCL injury in Week 11 ended his season. In the nine games Michael Penix Jr. started, London saw 94 of Penix's 166 attempts — a 56.6% target share, on a 17-game pace of 113-1,530-11. New HC Kevin Stefanski took over, Tua Tagovailoa was signed to compete, and Atlanta released last year's No. 2 WR Darnell Mooney, replacing him with Jahan Dotson, rookie Zachariah Branch and Olamide Zaccheaus. Kyle Pitts and Bijan Robinson remain the other mouths to feed.
- **Health:** Reported entering 2026 with a clean bill of health after the PCL injury. He has, however, missed time in four separate seasons across a four-year career.
- **Risk:** Quarterback and offensive quality. Stefanski had not named a Week 1 starter as of late August — Penix is coming off a partial ACL tear and Tagovailoa missed the start of camp. ESPN's power index has Atlanta as roughly the 22nd offense, projecting only 210–215 passing yards per game, which caps London's yardage even at a 30%+ share. Stefanski's system could also compress his target share.
- **Draft note:** ADP ~17 overall; Round 2 in half-PPR. He's a volume-and-talent bet with an offense-quality discount baked in — reasonable, not a bargain.
- **Confidence:** medium — his role is certain, but the Week 1 QB was still undeclared in the most recent reporting I found.
