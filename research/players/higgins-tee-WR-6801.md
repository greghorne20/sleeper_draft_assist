## Tee Higgins — WR, CIN, bye 6  (player_id: 6801)
- **Role:** starter (clear WR2 behind Ja'Marr Chase, with Andrei Iosivas as WR3)
- **Situation:** Higgins is back in a full-strength Bengals passing offense with Joe Burrow healthy after Burrow's 2025 toe injury forced Jake Browning and Joe Flacco into multiple starts. He put up 59 catches on 98 targets for 846 yards and a career-high 11 TDs in an injury-shortened 2025 and made his first Pro Bowl. Nothing meaningful changed around him this offseason: he is on the four-year, $115M deal signed in 2025, Chase is still the alpha, and Chase Brown and Mike Gesicki also work the passing game. His calling card is red-zone usage — 8th among WRs in end-zone targets and 4th in TDs over the last two seasons.
- **Health:** Heel contusion (bruised heel). Zac Taylor called it not a major concern on Sept. 1, but Higgins did not practice Wednesday Sept. 2 and is listed questionable. Taylor said he and Chase (hyperextended knee) should be ready for the Sept. 13 opener vs. Tampa Bay. He has missed seven games over the past two seasons.
- **Risk:** The specific failure mode is games missed. He has now had multiple soft-tissue/contact absences in back-to-back years, and the heel is unresolved a week out. Secondary risk: with Chase healthy and commanding the alpha share, Higgins' weekly ceiling is TD-dependent, and 11 TDs on 98 targets is not a repeatable rate.
- **Draft note:** In half-PPR his TD equity matters more than his catch volume, which pushes him up relative to full PPR. Feels like a solid WR2 you take in the back half of round 3 / early round 4 in a 12-teamer — you're paying for the Burrow offense, not for volume independence.
- **Confidence:** high (role and situation), medium on Week 1 availability — the heel is unresolved as of Sept. 3.
