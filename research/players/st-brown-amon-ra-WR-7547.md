## Amon-Ra St. Brown — WR, DET, bye 6  (player_id: 7547)
- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Jared Goff's most-trusted target and Detroit's clear No. 1, a top-3 fantasy WR three straight seasons who led the NFL in red-zone targets in 2025. New OC Drew Petzing is his fifth play-caller and St. Brown himself said the offense won't "click right away"; Jameson Williams remains the deep threat, with Isaac TeSlaa and Greg Dortch behind them.
- **Health:** Recovered from an offseason knee cleanup (postseason surgery) and back for a full camp; no current injury.
- **Risk:** Petzing's run-leaning tendencies trimming total targets during the scheme transition — otherwise one of the safest floors in fantasy.
- **Draft note:** Top-5 WR; the PPR-friendly target volume is a real asset even at half a point — worth a mid-to-late Round 1 pick.
- **Confidence:** high
