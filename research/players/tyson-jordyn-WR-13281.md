## Jordyn Tyson — WR, NO, bye 8  (player_id: 13281)

- **Role:** starter when healthy — but currently on IR with designation to return; will miss roughly the first month
- **Situation:** Tyson went No. 8 overall to New Orleans out of Arizona State and walked into a clean role opposite Chris Olave in Kellen Moore's offense with Tyler Shough at QB. He led the 2026 rookie class with a 35% target share during the nine weeks he was active in 2025 and posted a 2.73 YPRR (third in the class). Camp reports had him picking up the offense fast. Then a hamstring strain wiped out the back half of his summer — he missed the final three weeks of camp and the entire preseason — and the Saints placed him on IR with a return designation in late August.
- **Health:** Right hamstring, recurring. This is the same hamstring that cost him the end of his 2025 Arizona State season, the Combine and his Pro Day; he was also on a maintenance plan through OTAs and minicamp for it. The current flare-up is expected to cost roughly two months on the sideline. He also has a 2022 knee injury and a 2024 broken collarbone in his history — durability was reportedly the biggest pre-draft concern on him.
- **Risk:** He's a rookie who will have missed camp, the preseason and the start of the year, which is far more costly for a first-year player learning an offense and building QB rapport than for a veteran. Even after activation, a soft-tissue injury that has recurred this many times can recur again, and the Saints have every reason to bring him back slowly behind an established Olave.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR this is a stash, not a starter — you are paying an early-season roster spot for maybe 11–12 games of a rookie ramping up. With only 6 bench slots in this league that cost is real; he's a last-round or waiver-wire target rather than a mid-round pick, and more attractive if your league has an IR slot (config doesn't show one).
- **Confidence:** medium (injury and return window are well documented; post-return role and volume are not)
