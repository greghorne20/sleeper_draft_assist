## Ladd McConkey — WR, LAC, bye 7  (player_id: 11635)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** The Chargers' WR1 with Keenan Allen gone, working in Mike McDaniel's new offense alongside Quentin Johnston, Tre Harris and TE Oronde Gadsden. His rookie 2024 was excellent (82-1,149-7); 2025 was a clear step back, and the underlying numbers are the reason to be careful — 29th in target share (19.8%), 62nd in yards per route run (1.47), 37th in receiving yards per game (49.3) and 46th in first-read share (20.4%). The bull case is environmental: McDaniel replacing Greg Roman, and Rashawn Slater and Joe Alt returning to a line that allowed a league-high pressure rate on Herbert. He's expected to be the go-to on key downs and in the red zone.
- **Health:** Strained his left hamstring during OTAs in June, was held out of 11-on-11 work at minicamp, and declared himself a "full go" at the start of training camp on July 29, saying he got healthy over the summer. I did not find a health update more recent than early August, so his current status is inferred from the absence of new injury reporting rather than confirmed.
- **Risk:** He was a low-efficiency, low-first-read WR3 by the numbers last season, and a scheme change doesn't guarantee that reverses. Add a soft-tissue injury he's already had once this year in a Harbaugh offense that has averaged around 32 pass attempts per game, and the realistic downside is another WR30-ish season at a WR2 price.
- **Draft note:** A bet on the offensive environment rather than the player's 2025 production; in half-PPR his short-area volume is worth less than in full PPR, so he's a player to take at a discount to his mid-WR2 ADP rather than at it.
- **Confidence:** medium — role and offseason are well documented, but I could not confirm his health status past early August
