## George Kittle — TE, SF, bye 8  (player_id: 4217)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** When on the field he's still elite — 57-628-7 in 11 games last year, tied for TE2 in fantasy points per game, with a 22.8% target share (third among TEs) and the best catchable-target rate at the position (88.4%). San Francisco's pass-catching group churned: Mike Evans signed in free agency, Deebo Samuel returned, De'Zhaun Stribling was drafted, and Ricky Pearsall is out for the season. Evans and Stribling have both had their own injury issues in camp, which could push a big early target share Kittle's way. Brock Purdy is healthy and should be his primary QB. Kittle turns 33 on Oct. 9.
- **Health:** This is the whole evaluation. He tore his right Achilles on Jan. 11 in the wild-card win at Philadelphia. He was activated off PUP on Aug. 23 (which kept him off the reserve list that would have ruled him out through Week 4), ramped up through non-competitive periods, and as of Sept. 2 Ian Rapoport reported he traveled with the team to Melbourne after two positive practice days and is "trending in the right direction to play." Shanahan said he wanted the call made before the flight rather than send Kittle on a trip he couldn't finish. The opener is Thursday, Sept. 10 vs. the Rams — one day short of eight months post-tear, and a Thursday, so three fewer recovery days than a normal Week 1. Not confirmed active; a snap count or pitch count is plausible.
- **Risk:** Either he isn't ready and you're starting a hole in Week 1, or he plays on a limited count and the explosiveness that drives his YAC profile isn't there yet. Achilles recoveries at 32–33 frequently look fine in September and better in November. Backup fallout: Jake Tonges (and possibly Luke Farrell) if he sits.
- **Draft note:** ADP around pick 96–97 (Round 9) is a real discount on a top-three tight end. In half-PPR take him there and pair him with a second cheap TE — Kincaid, Goedert, Likely — so you're covered for the first month. The payoff case is him at full strength for the fantasy playoffs.
- **Confidence:** medium
