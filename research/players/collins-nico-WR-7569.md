## Nico Collins — WR, HOU, bye 8  (player_id: 7569)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** The X receiver and unambiguous No. 1 in Houston, with the depth chart behind him in flux: Xavier Hutchinson at Z, Jaylin Noel in the slot, and Kayshon Boutte (acquired via trade from New England) in reserve. The defining offseason change is that Jayden Higgins, who had been set to start opposite him, tore his ACL in practice and is out for the season, and Tank Dell hasn't played since 2024 and will miss at least the first four games. That points to a target share meaningfully above the 24.6% he ran last year. Collins caught 71 of 120 for 1,117 and six TDs in 15 games in 2025, a third straight 1,000-yard season, with C.J. Stroud back at QB.
- **Health:** No current injury. His limited offseason work was rest, not injury, per his own comments to NFL.com. History is the concern: he has never played a full 17-game season, missed five games in 2024 with a hamstring, and suffered a severe concussion in the 2025 wild-card round that kept him out of the divisional game.
- **Risk:** Availability. He's the most concentrated target in a thin room, which is exactly why any missed time is catastrophic to the pick — and he has missed time every single year. Secondary risk is Stroud regressing again with no complementary threat forcing coverage away.
- **Draft note:** A high-target, high-red-zone WR1 profile with real injury tax; in half-PPR his yardage and TD equity carry him, and he's worth a top-15 overall look but not a reach above his ADP.
- **Confidence:** high
