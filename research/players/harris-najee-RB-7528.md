## Najee Harris — RB, NYG, bye 8  (player_id: 7528)
- **Role:** committee (currently co-No. 2 in a four-back room)
- **Situation:** Signed a one-year deal with the Giants in mid-August 2026 after the Chargers released/moved on following his torn Achilles in Week 3 of 2025. New Giants HC is John Harbaugh, who pushed for him. Cam Skattebo is the clear lead back; Harris, Tyrone Tracy Jr. and Devin Singletary are behind him, and the Giants kept all four on the initial 53. Harris was the first back to take a snap in the preseason finale (11 carries, 39 yards, plus a 20-yard catch) with Skattebo and Singletary resting. Reported as co-No. 2 on the depth chart.
- **Health:** Recovering from a torn Achilles suffered Week 3, 2025 (missed the rest of that season). He acknowledged at signing that he was not yet in football shape. No injury designation reported since; he played in the preseason finale.
- **Risk:** Four backs, one ball. Skattebo is the entrenched starter and Harris is splitting the leftovers with Tracy and Singletary, so he can easily be a zero-touch inactive most weeks. He also has never been an efficient runner (3.9 career YPC) and is coming off an Achilles at age 28 with a brand-new offense.
- **Draft note:** Half-PPR handcuff/bench dart only. In a 12-team league with 13 rounds and one flex there is essentially no room for him — leave him on the wire and claim him the week Skattebo gets hurt. If your league were deeper, last-round territory.
- **Confidence:** medium
