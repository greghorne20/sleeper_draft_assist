## Travis Hunter — WR, JAX, bye 7  (player_id: 12530)

- **Role:** committee / situational on offense — a two-way player whose primary 2026 role appears to be cornerback, with game-plan-specific receiver snaps. Listed as a backup WR on the depth chart.
- **Situation:** The No. 2 pick of the 2025 draft played 66%+ of his rookie snaps on offense and was breaking out — 8 catches for 101 yards and a TD vs. the Rams in Week 7, while covering Davante Adams well in the same game — before a right-knee injury (reported as an isolated LCL/ICL tear) in an Oct. 30 practice ended his season after 7 games (28-298-1 on 45 targets). Liam Coen insists "the plan has not changed" on two-way usage and NFL Network reported Jacksonville intends to use him as their top WR and matchup corner. But training camp reporting has him practicing almost exclusively at cornerback, and the roster math argues the same way: Brian Thomas Jr., Jakobi Meyers, Parker Washington and TE Brenton Strange are all healthy, and the defense needs him. He also missed the entire offseason program rehabbing.
- **Health:** Returned to practice in training camp after the season-ending knee surgery. No current designation found, but he had no offseason program reps and is roughly ten months post-surgery.
- **Risk:** He may simply not get enough offensive snaps to matter. He's fourth or fifth in the route order behind Thomas, Meyers, Washington and Strange, he had zero touchdowns on five red-zone targets as a rookie, and if Jacksonville leans him toward corner the receiver work becomes a handful of schemed touches per game. Reinjury on a knee that hasn't been tested in a full workload is the second risk.
- **Draft note:** A last-round upside stash in 0.5 PPR — ADP is around 161 and he's ranked anywhere from WR58 to WR73. Take him in round 13 or off waivers, not before.
- **Confidence:** medium (the offensive snap share is genuinely unresolved and sources conflict — the team says two-way, camp reports say cornerback)
