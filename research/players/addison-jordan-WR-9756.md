## Jordan Addison — WR, MIN, bye 6  (player_id: 9756)

- **Role:** starter (clear WR2 behind Justin Jefferson)
- **Situation:** Addison is the Vikings' No. 2 receiver and took regular first-team reps throughout camp. The big offseason change is at quarterback: Kyler Murray is Minnesota's new starter, replacing the J.J. McCarthy/Carson Wentz/Max Brosmer mess that wrecked the entire pass game in 2025 (Addison fell to 42-610-3 in 14 games, a 17.4% target share, 43.6 yards per game and 9.7 PPG — WR43/44). Addison and Murray trained together in Dallas for roughly two months this offseason and both reported to camp early. Minnesota picked up his fifth-year option ($18M guaranteed for 2027), so the team is still invested.
- **Health:** Healthy. He jammed his thumb in early August; imaging came back clean, he missed a day or two of catching passes and returned to full work. He was rested for the final two preseason games and is expected to be ready for Week 1 vs. Green Bay.
- **Risk:** The bounce-back may be smaller than the QB upgrade implies. Murray is coming off a five-game, foot-injury season in Arizona and is a "functional" starter rather than an offense-elevator; the Vikings ranked 26th in scoring and ran 54.1% pass in 2025. Addison's underlying 2025 metrics (1.53 YPRR, 22.1% first-read share) were not just quarterback-driven, and Jefferson will always cap the target ceiling.
- **Draft note:** A WR3/flex bet on situation improvement in 0.5 PPR — reasonable around WR38–44 (roughly rounds 8–10), which is close to his current market price, so you're not getting a discount for the risk.
- **Confidence:** high
