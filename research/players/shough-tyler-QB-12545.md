## Tyler Shough — QB, NO, bye 8  (player_id: 12545)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** ESPN reported New Orleans is moving ahead with Shough as the 2026 starter, and he's described as having full confidence with a long leash given who's behind him. The 2025 second-rounder took over from Spencer Rattler in Week 9 and went 5-4 across the final nine games: 2,384 yards, 10 TD, 6 INT overall, 67.6% completions, 7.3 YPA, plus 195 rushing yards and three scores. He was QB12 in PPG from Week 9 on and QB5 from Weeks 12–18, with 17.1+ fantasy points in each of his last six starts. Kellen Moore's system is fantasy-friendly and fast-paced — the Saints ranked 8th in overall pass rate (61.3%) and 5th in neutral-situation pass rate with Shough, and he was 4th among QBs in designed rushing yards during his starting sample and 9th in deep throw rate. New Orleans added Travis Etienne Jr. and drafted WR Jordyn Tyson No. 8 overall to pair with Chris Olave.
- **Health:** No injury designation found for Shough. The relevant health note is his supporting cast: Tyson has a hamstring injury and is reportedly expected to miss roughly the next two months, which pushes early-season volume onto Olave.
- **Risk:** The nine-start sample was soft. Only one of the defenses he faced (the Rams) ranked top-10 in pass DVOA, and he's a 26-year-old rookie-season profile with essentially no historical comps. New Orleans was also 28th in red-zone pass rate over expected, which capped his 3.1% TD rate (tied 31st). Without Tyson, the early-season passing game may not support QB1 weeks at all.
- **Draft note:** ADP around QB20–21 (roughly pick 150) is cheap for someone with genuine top-12 upside once Tyson is back. In a 4-point-TD, 1-QB league, take him Round 13+ as the second half of a QB tandem with Mayfield-type streaming value, not as your only starter.
- **Confidence:** high
