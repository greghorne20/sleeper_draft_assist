## Rico Dowdle — RB, PIT, bye 9  (player_id: 7021)

- **Role:** starter / lead early-down back in a two-back committee with Jaylen Warren
- **Situation:** Dowdle signed a two-year, $12.25M deal with Pittsburgh specifically for a shot at RB1 work, reuniting with Mike McCarthy from their Dallas days. He's coming off back-to-back 1,000-yard rushing seasons (1,328 total yards and 5 TDs on 274 touches in Dallas 2024; 1,373 and 7 TDs in Carolina 2025) and was the RB10 in points per game from Weeks 5–18 last year at 18.6 touches and 87.7 total yards per game. The camp signals conflicted — the initial team depth chart listed Warren ahead of him even though beat reporters had Dowdle taking the majority of first-team reps — but the picture has clarified: Mark Kaboly wrote Dowdle "will be the unquestioned RB1 when the season starts," and ESPN's Dan Graziano reported Dowdle is the likelier early-down back. Pittsburgh traded Kaleb Johnson to Green Bay and cut Travis Homer and Lew Nichols, leaving only Warren and Dowdle listed strictly at RB on the 53. Aaron Rodgers is the QB.
- **Health:** No current injury reported. He has an older injury history (2021 hip, 2022 ankle) but has taken 471 carries over the last two seasons.
- **Risk:** Warren takes the receiving work — he has 139 catches over the last three years — which caps Dowdle's ceiling in a half-PPR format. Dowdle has had exactly 39 receptions in each of the last two seasons and that number is expected to fall. If the split ends up closer to even than reported, he's a touchdown-dependent RB3 rather than an RB2. McCarthy is also a first-year coach here with no track record in this building.
- **Draft note:** A low-end RB2 / high-end RB3 in this scoring — the reduced pass-game role costs him more in 0.5 PPR than it would in standard. Reasonable in the fifth-to-seventh round; the profile is "safe volume, limited ceiling."
- **Confidence:** medium-high (workload direction is well sourced but the official depth chart still contradicts it)
