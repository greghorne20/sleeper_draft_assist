## Oronde Gadsden — TE, LAC, bye 7  (player_id: 12493)

- **Role:** committee
- **Situation:** (Rostered as Oronde Gadsden II.) A 2025 fifth-round pick who was a genuine surprise as a rookie: 49-664-3 on 69 targets in 15 games (seven starts), TE17 overall, and the most explosive tight end in the league by yards per reception (13.6, first among TEs with 50+ targets). The offseason went against him. Los Angeles signed David Njoku and Charlie Kolar, and the first unofficial depth chart listed all three as co-starters with "or" designations. Gadsden has been candid about needing to improve as a blocker, which is exactly where Njoku and Kolar are stronger, and he reportedly played mostly with the backups in preseason. Mike McDaniel is the new offensive coordinator — the reason for the early-offseason hype, since he got a productive season out of Darren Waller — but the target competition also includes Ladd McConkey, Quentin Johnston and Tre' Harris.
- **Health:** No injury designation found. He played in preseason (two catches, 24 yards vs. San Francisco; two for 23 vs. Denver).
- **Risk:** A three-way tight end rotation. His 61.4% snap share was already only 28th in the league last year and he failed to clear a 70% route share in any game after Week 15. If Njoku takes the base-down role and Kolar takes the blocking snaps, Gadsden is a situational pass-catcher whose entire rookie fantasy value came from a four-game hot stretch in Weeks 6–9. Take that month away and there's very little.
- **Draft note:** In a 1-TE half-PPR league he's a late-round dart or a waiver-wire watch, not a starter — Round 13 or leave him. Rankings on him range widely (TE20 to TE31), which is itself the signal.
- **Confidence:** medium
