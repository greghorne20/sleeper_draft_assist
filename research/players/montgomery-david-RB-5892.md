## David Montgomery — RB, HOU, bye 8  (player_id: 5892)

- **Role:** starter (lead back in a two-man committee)
- **Situation:** Traded from Detroit to Houston this offseason for Juice Scruggs, a 4th and a 7th, then given a new deal with a $6.5M signing bonus. He is the clear early-down/RB1 with second-year back Woody Marks — the 2025 team rushing leader — sliding into the pass-catching/satellite role he's better suited for. Houston released Joe Mixon and let Nick Chubb walk, upgraded the offensive line in the draft and free agency, and showed run-heavy intent after finishing 29th in yards per carry in 2025. RBs coach Danny Barrett has called him a three-down back, and OC Nick Caley has floated split-back sets with Montgomery and Marks together.
- **Health:** No injury designation reported in any coverage reviewed through early September. He played every regular-season game in 2025 and told Houston media in August he's "in the best shape of my life." Nothing lingering surfaced in searches.
- **Risk:** He's a touchdown-dependent flex whose receiving usage is minimal — his target share in Detroit fell each year, and Marks is the designated passing-down back. Houston's offense is a real downgrade from the 2025 Lions (13th in offense vs. the best season in Lions history). If the TDs regress or Marks eats into early downs, there's no yardage or reception floor underneath. Age-29 back on a team with an elite defense that may also just grind clock without him scoring.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR with 2RB+FLEX, he's a fine RB2/high-end flex in the mid-rounds — the half-point-per-reception setting hurts him relative to full PPR since he doesn't catch passes, but 6 pts/TD and volume in a run-leaning offense keeps his floor real. Take him where RB2s go, not before.
- **Confidence:** medium
