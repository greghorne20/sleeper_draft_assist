## Javonte Williams — RB, DAL, bye 14  (player_id: 7588)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** The clearest RB1 situation in the batch. Dallas waived Jaydon Blue, Phil Mafah and Israel Abanikanda during cuts, leaving Malik Davis and waiver claim Emari Demercado as the only backs behind him. Williams did not play in the preseason at all, which ESPN's Todd Archer reads as the team being fully comfortable with his standing. He signed a three-year extension (about $8M per year) after a breakout 2025 and is described as having workhorse style with enough receiving and blocking ability to stay on the field. Same offense as last year — Dak Prescott, Brian Schottenheimer in year two, Klayton Adams calling plays — with the same 11 offensive starters returning.
- **Health:** No reported injury. He tore up his knee in 2022 but has missed only one game across the two seasons since.
- **Risk:** Workload management rather than competition. FantasyPros reports Dallas doesn't want to give him the same volume he carried in 2025, and Demercado was specifically claimed because he'd worked with Adams in Arizona — if the coaching staff throttles him back to preserve him, the ceiling shrinks fast. His underlying efficiency was also poor for two straight years in Denver, so a repeat of last year's touchdown rate isn't guaranteed.
- **Draft note:** Volume and touchdown equity in a top-scoring offense with essentially no handcuff behind him; the third round is where he's being taken and that's a reasonable price for a floor pick in a 2RB+FLEX league.
- **Confidence:** high
