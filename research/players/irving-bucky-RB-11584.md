## Bucky Irving — RB, TB, bye 10  (player_id: 11584)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Lead back, cleanly ahead of Kenny Gainwell and Sean Tucker. Rachaad White left in free agency and Tampa signed Gainwell, a pass-catching specialist who will take some passing-down targets. Zac Robinson is the offensive coordinator and made a point of getting Irving the ball on each of the Buccaneers' first three plays in his preseason cameo. His 2024 rookie year was 207 carries for 1,122 and eight TDs plus 47 catches for 392; 2025 collapsed to 588 rushing yards and one rushing TD on 173 carries across 10 games because of a shoulder subluxation and a foot sprain, with 3.4 yards per carry when he did play.
- **Health:** Recovered. He had offseason shoulder surgery and there was real doubt in May when Todd Bowles said "summer or fall," but he returned at OTAs in June and Bowles declared him a "full-go" with no limitations at the start of camp in late July. He logged three carries in the preseason game against Kansas City. Fantasy Football Calculator lists no current injury.
- **Risk:** The shoulder. It's the specific injury that ruined last season, it required surgery, and a contact-heavy runner testing it under pads is the thing that would make this pick fail. Secondary: Gainwell erodes the receiving work that gives Irving his half-PPR floor, and the presence of two backups gives Tampa an easy path to managing his workload.
- **Draft note:** A bounce-back RB2 with RB1 upside on a full workload; the discount from the injury year is where the value is, but he needs the receiving role to stay intact to justify a top-24 pick in this scoring.
- **Confidence:** high
