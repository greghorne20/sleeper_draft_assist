## Breece Hall — RB, NYJ, bye 13  (player_id: 8155)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Lead back on a new three-year, $45.75M extension signed in May, with Braelon Allen as the clear No. 2 and Isaiah Davis third. SNY reported in August that his snap share is expected to rise from 61.1% in 2025 (down from 67.5% in 2024). New OC Frank Reich has said the Jets want to emphasize the run, and Geno Smith is the quarterback. The receiving role is the soft spot: Hall's 2025 line was 243 carries for 1,065 and four TDs but only 36 catches on roughly three targets per game, down from 76 catches in 2023, with Davis taking third-down work — a split CBS notes Reich has historically used.
- **Health:** Dealt with a groin injury during training camp; NewYorkJets.com reporting cited by FantasyData has him expected back in the starting backfield for the Week 1 game against Tennessee. Isaiah Davis has also been dealing with a knee issue.
- **Risk:** Touchdown and receiving suppression. Allen is a 240-pound back who has been scoring in red-zone work, and head coach Aaron Glenn has repeatedly said he wants a three-back rotation. Hall needs volume to pay off on a bad offense, and this is a coaching staff that has shown it will spread the touches.
- **Draft note:** Half-PPR softens the loss of receiving work relative to full PPR, which helps him a little; a fair RB2 in the RB15–20 range, but the goal-line share is the thing that determines whether the pick works.
- **Confidence:** high
