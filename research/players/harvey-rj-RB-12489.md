## RJ Harvey — RB, DEN, bye 10  (player_id: 12489)

- **Role:** committee — specifically the passing-downs/third-down back, behind J.K. Dobbins on early downs
- **Situation:** Denver re-signed Dobbins, who sits atop the depth chart, and drafted Jonah Coleman, who has reportedly been excellent this preseason as a between-the-tackles runner. Sean Payton has said Harvey "certainly" will be the third-down back and praised his "natural hands." Harvey's rookie year split cleanly: through 10 games he had five or fewer carries seven times with Dobbins dominating; after Dobbins' season-ending foot injury he was RB13 in PPG from Weeks 11-18 on 16.8 touches and 60.7% of snaps, but inefficiently (3.4 YPC over the final seven regular-season games, 3.0 in two playoff games). Full rookie line: 540 rushing yards and 7 TDs, plus 47 catches on 58 targets for 356 yards and 5 more TDs — 896 scrimmage yards and 12 total TDs. Note he was listed fifth on the initial preseason depth chart despite repping as the No. 2; beat writers say Payton routinely buries young players on those charts.
- **Health:** No current injury reported. A shoulder issue was referenced in mid-July reporting but he has been practicing since; nothing active surfaced.
- **Risk:** The math doesn't repeat. Forty percent of his fantasy points came from receiving and another 35% from touchdowns, on only a 10.1% target share and a 45% opportunity share. He was 40th of 49 in rush gain rate, 42nd in explosive run rate and 44th in yards after contact per attempt at 3.7 YPC. If the touchdown rate normalizes and the volume doesn't grow — and with both Dobbins and Coleman in the room, it probably won't — there's very little underneath. Even a Dobbins injury may now funnel carries to Coleman rather than Harvey.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR his receptions are worth half what they'd be in full PPR, which is a real hit to a player whose value is receiving-driven — his RB32-36 range is where he belongs here, maybe a touch later. Round 9+ flex dart, not a target.
- **Confidence:** medium
