## Jaydon Blue — RB, PHI, bye 10  (player_id: 12457)

- **Role:** camp body — practice squad, not on the active roster
- **Situation:** Important correction to the batch file's team: Blue is no longer a Cowboy. Dallas waived him at the 53-man cutdown after he failed to win their backup running back job, then didn't sign him back to their own practice squad. Philadelphia signed him to their practice squad on September 1 as part of their initial 17. He is behind Saquon Barkley, Tank Bigsby and Will Shipley on the active roster and behind or alongside Dameon Pierce and Carson Steele on the practice squad — six running backs deep in the organization. Brian Schottenheimer noted his maturity had improved in 2026, which wasn't enough to keep the roster spot. He's a fifth-round pick from 2025 out of Texas who ran a 4.38 forty, second-fastest among backs at that combine.
- **Health:** No injury designation found; he was waived for performance and roster-math reasons, not health.
- **Risk:** He isn't on an NFL active roster. He'd need multiple injuries ahead of him just to be elevated for a game, and his rookie production gives no reason to think he'd hold a job if elevated — 38 carries for 129 yards (3.4 per carry) and one TD in five games, plus one catch for five yards. He is a special-teams and depth body on a team that has an established bell cow.
- **Draft note:** Do not draft. There is no version of a 12-team, 13-round redraft where a practice-squad RB6 is a defensible use of a pick. Not even a watch-list name unless Barkley goes down and Blue is promoted over Bigsby and Shipley, which would be two surprises stacked.
