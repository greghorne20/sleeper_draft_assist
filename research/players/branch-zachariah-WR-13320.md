## Zachariah Branch — WR, ATL, bye 11  (player_id: 13320)

- **Role:** backup / rotational rookie with a real return job — projected WR4 on offense, likely the primary punt returner
- **Situation:** Third-round pick (No. 79 overall) out of Georgia, and the most consistently praised player in Atlanta's offseason. Tua Tagovailoa compared him to Jaylen Waddle in the spring; The Athletic's Josh Kendall called him the most noticeable player on the field all offseason. The Falcons plan to line him up at all three receiver spots, but the reported quickest path to snaps is the F/slot job, where veteran Olamide Zaccheaus was still taking most of the first-team reps. On the current depth chart he projects fourth behind Drake London, Jahan Dotson and Zaccheaus. He won the Jet Award as the nation's top return specialist and returned a punt 18 yards in the preseason win over Miami, though he played only four total snaps that game. Big context item: Atlanta had not named a Week 1 starting QB as of early September — Tagovailoa has taken the majority of first-team reps all offseason while Michael Penix Jr. worked back from a third career ACL tear, and Kevin Stefanski, GM Ian Cunningham, football president Matt Ryan and Arthur Blank are all involved in the decision.
- **Health:** No injury designation found. He's on the active roster and played in the preseason.
- **Risk:** He's fourth in line on offense and the profile is small-and-shifty — 5'9", 177, with a 5.2 aDOT across 37 college games and no season above 700 yards or four TDs at USC or Georgia. His likely 2026 is 25 catches on screens and jet motion plus return yards your league doesn't score. Beyond London, none of these Falcons receivers has been productive, which is exactly why nobody has been forced to give Branch a job. The unsettled QB room is a second layer of risk on top.
- **Draft note:** Final-round flier only, and mostly in leagues deeper than yours. Half-PPR helps a high-catch, low-aDOT slot player more than standard would, so if he does win the F job his profile fits your scoring reasonably. But at 12 teams and 13 rounds, spend the pick on someone with a defined role and add Branch off waivers when the slot job resolves.
- **Confidence:** medium — the draft position, camp buzz and depth-chart placement are all well sourced; his actual Week 1 role is not settled and neither is his quarterback.
