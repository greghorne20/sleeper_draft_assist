## Joe Burrow — QB, CIN, bye 6  (player_id: 6770)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Burrow returns as Cincinnati's franchise passer with arguably the league's best receiver pair in Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins. He played only eight games in 2025 (1,809 yards, 17 TD, 5 INT) after turf toe required surgery; Joe Flacco covered in relief and the offense still ranked 10th in TD rate per drive. The Bengals kept O-line continuity as a stated point of emphasis (all five starters return), and there's reporting they want Burrow under center more often to open the play-action game. Cincinnati's defense was last in yards allowed per play (6.2), which keeps them in shootouts.
- **Health:** Reported at full health this summer. He played the first two drives of the preseason opener (5-of-6, 39 yards) and sat the finale with the rest of the starters, which is normal rest, not a flag.
- **Risk:** Availability. Three separate multi-week absences in four years (2020 knee, 2023 wrist, 2025 turf toe) is a pattern, not variance. Secondary risk specific to your scoring: Burrow got 2.9% of his fantasy points from rushing, so with 4-point passing TDs and 0.04/yard he has almost no floor beneath his passing volume — 76.5% of Bengals TDs came through the air last year, and modest TD regression hits him hard.
- **Draft note:** In a 1-QB league with 4-point passing TDs, he's a mid-round QB1 — roughly Rounds 6–8. Your scoring actively penalizes the pocket-passer archetype, so I wouldn't pay the QB3-QB4 national ADP here.
- **Confidence:** high
