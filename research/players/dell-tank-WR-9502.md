## Tank Dell — WR, HOU, bye 8  (player_id: 9502)

- **Role:** UNKNOWN — on injured reserve to open the season; no role has been established for whenever he returns
- **Situation:** Houston placed Dell on IR with a designation to return at the August 30 cutdown, so he cannot play before Week 5 (his first eligible game is the divisional matchup with Tennessee, with one outlet listing an estimated return of 10/11/2026). GM Nick Caserio said in early September that Dell is "close" but "not quite there," and closer than he was in camp. Dell also took a pay cut on the final year of his rookie deal, down from $3.624M to just over $1.7M with $1M guaranteed. The opportunity is real if he gets healthy: Jayden Higgins tore his ACL in a joint practice with the Raiders and is out for the year, leaving Nico Collins, Xavier Hutchinson, Jaylin Noel and trade acquisition Kayshon Boutte in the room. He caught 105+ targets' worth of production as a 2023 rookie and averaged 3.6 catches for 47.6 yards per game in 2024 before the injury.
- **Health:** Recovering from a catastrophic December 2024 knee injury — ACL, MCL, LCL and meniscus damage plus a dislocated kneecap, requiring surgeries in January and March 2025. He missed all of 2025. He avoided the PUP list at the start of camp, which was encouraging, but the camp reality was thin: mostly individual drills, only two team sessions, only one of those in pads, and he did not practice in either joint session against Carolina or Las Vegas. His last 11-on-11 work was August 10.
- **Risk:** Almost everything. He's 20+ months removed from an injury that destroys explosiveness, he's 5'10" and 165 pounds and depended entirely on separation quickness, and he barely practiced in pads all summer. Even the optimistic path costs you four roster weeks before you learn anything. The specific failure: he returns in Week 6, looks a step slow, and settles in as the fourth option behind Collins, Hutchinson and Noel.
- **Draft note:** In a 13-round league with six bench spots, an IR-designated player who can't help you until Week 5 and may not help you at all is not worth a pick — you'd be burning a bench slot for a month on a coin flip. Let someone else take him and monitor the Week 5 activation. If your league has an IR slot that doesn't count against the 6 bench spots, that changes the math and he becomes a defensible final-round stash.
- **Confidence:** high on the injury and roster facts (multiple current sources agree); low on any projection of role or production, which nobody can currently make.
