## Rashee Rice — WR, KC, bye 5  (player_id: 10229)

- **Role:** starter
- **Situation:** Kansas City's primary target when on the field. His 2025 target share was 28.7% (26.2% by another accounting), with roughly 30% of his routes drawing a target and over half his routes run at or near the line of scrimmage — a high-volume underneath role Andy Reid schemes for him. Target competition is thin: Xavier Worthy managed only a 15.0% share in games Rice played, Travis Kelce is aging, and the rest is unproven. The new wrinkle is RB Kenneth Walker, a genuinely capable receiving back who could take some of the checkdown volume. He averaged 18.8 fantasy PPG in 2025 and 21.6 in his three healthy 2024 games.
- **Health:** Had surgery in May on his right knee — the same knee in which he tore the LCL in 2024 — to remove loose debris, with roughly a two-month timeline. Arrowhead Pride reported he was leaving camp practices early each day for planned rehab, so he was not fully recovered mid-summer. Multiple outlets report he is expected to be ready for Week 1. Patrick Mahomes is himself returning from a torn ACL and by all accounts is also ready.
- **Risk:** Availability, full stop. He has played 12 games total across the last two seasons between the knee injury and a suspension, and this offseason included a knee scope plus 30 days in jail for a probation violation. A recurrence of the knee issue, or any further discipline, ends the pick. The Mahomes ACL return is a stacked second risk on the same asset.
- **Draft note:** A WR1 role at a WR12-ish price with WR30 risk of games missed; in half-PPR his short-area volume is worth slightly less than in full PPR, so take him where the discount is real rather than paying the full ADP.
- **Confidence:** high on role; medium on the health picture, since the most specific recent detail is that he was still doing planned rehab during camp
