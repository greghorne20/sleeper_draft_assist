## Calvin Ridley — WR, TEN, bye 9  (player_id: 4981)

- **Role:** starter (outside), but in a genuine rotation — closer to co-starter than entrenched WR1
- **Situation:** Tennessee gutted and rebuilt this room around him. They drafted Carnell Tate No. 4 overall, signed Wan'Dale Robinson away from the Giants, and kept second-year men Chimere Dike and Elic Ayomanor; Ridley is one of five WRs on the initial 53 and the only one besides Robinson with more than a year of NFL experience. Reporting has him competing for the outside job opposite Tate with Robinson working mainly from the slot. The staff is new top to bottom — HC Robert Saleh, OC Brian Daboll (who was Ridley's OC at Alabama in 2017), new WR coach Greg Lewis — running a new offense for second-year QB Cam Ward. Tennessee restructured Ridley's deal to keep him, which is a real signal about how the new staff values him.
- **Health:** Reported fully recovered. He broke his right fibula on the Titans' first offensive snap against Houston on Nov. 16, 2025, ending his season at seven games — and that came in his first game back from a three-game hamstring absence. He played in the preseason (one catch, 18 yards vs. San Francisco). No current designation found.
- **Risk:** He's 31, coming off a lost year, and is the veteran in a room the franchise just spent a top-five pick and free-agent money to replace. If Tate arrives ready, this becomes a Tate/Robinson offense and Ridley is the third mouth on a unit that ranked 30th in passing yards and 31st in passing TDs a year ago. The floor here is 45 catches for 550 yards with the target share bleeding out by midseason.
- **Draft note:** In 0.5 PPR with only six bench spots, he's a late-round WR4/5 dart — the kind of name you take in the last two or three rounds because the outcome distribution is wide, not because you expect to start him. Don't reach for the name recognition.
- **Confidence:** medium — role is confirmed as a starter, but the size of the role is genuinely unsettled and depends on a rookie's development.
